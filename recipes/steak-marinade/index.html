Steak Marinade

Soy sauce, Worcestershire sauce, garlic, olive oil and black pepper whisked into a savoury marinade for steak. Four servings in 5 minutes.

Why Steak Marinade Works

A good marinade seasons the steak and helps it brown, and it takes no more than a whisk. Soy sauce brings salt and savour, Worcestershire sauce adds tang, and oil carries the flavour into the meat.

A little acid tenderises, but too much turns the surface mushy, so the marinade uses only a splash of vinegar. Leave steaks in it for 30 minutes to 4 hours, and no longer.

Pat the steak dry before it hits the pan. A wet surface steams, and the marinade that has soaked in does its work best against a dry, hot pan that sears.

Sugar in the marinade helps with colour but burns quickly, so use a medium-high heat and not the highest. The quantities below suit about 800 g of steak. Do not reuse the marinade as a sauce unless you boil it first. A spoon of Dijon mustard stirred in adds body and helps the marinade cling to the meat, if you like a sharper edge.

What to Put on the Table With Steak Marinade

  • Sirloin steak
  • Rump steak
  • Chips
  • Green salad

Making Steak Marinade, Step by Step

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Whisk the soy sauce, Worcestershire sauce, oil, garlic, vinegar, sugar and pepper in a bowl until the sugar dissolves.

  2. Lay the steak in a shallow dish, pour over the marinade and turn to coat. Cover and chill until cooking.

Worth Knowing Before You Cook

Marinate for no more than 4 hours.

Pat the steak dry before cooking.

Use a medium-high heat so the sugar does not burn.

Boil the leftover marinade before using it as a sauce.

Common Substitutions & Variations

  • An eighth of a teaspoon of garlic powder per clove is the usual stand-in when you are out of fresh garlic — it will not caramelise the same way, but it carries the flavour.

If You Need to Adjust It

  • Tamari is the direct swap for soy sauce if you need the dish gluten-free — it is brewed the same way, just without the wheat.
  • For a vegetarian version, look for a plant-based Worcestershire sauce — the anchovies in the standard bottle are what makes ordinary worcestershire sauce unsuitable, not anything else in it.

Keeping What's Left

Keeps in the fridge for up to 3 days before the meat goes in.
