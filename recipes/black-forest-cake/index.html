Black Forest Cake

Chocolate sponge soaked in kirsch, layered with sour cherries and unsweetened whipped cream, finished with chocolate curls. The kirsch is not optional in Germany — it is legally part of the definition.

1 hr 20 min prep and cooking plus 4 hr chilling plus time for cooling completely, which the recipe does not time at least 5 hr 20 min start to finish Serves 12 servings Hard

Why This Is Worth Making

The cream is barely sweetened because the sponge is already soaked in syrup and the cherries carry their own sugar — sweet cream would make the whole cake cloying. Soaking the layers with kirsch syrup while they are still slightly warm allows deeper absorption without turning them to mush.

What Goes With It

  • A glass of kirsch, or strong coffee
  • Serve as a celebration cake
  • Follows a hearty German meal

Making Black Forest Cake, Step by Step

Before you begin

  • Plan for 4 hr of chilling. It is part of the 5 hr 20 min start to finish.
  • The method also needs time for cooling completely, which it does not give, so allow extra.
  • You will need: oven.

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Line and grease three 20 cm tins. Heat the oven to 175°C / 345°F.

  2. Whisk the egg yolks with 120 g of the sugar for 4 minutes until pale and thick.

  3. In a clean bowl, whisk the whites with the salt to soft peaks, then add the remaining 60 g of sugar and whisk to firm, glossy peaks.

  4. Sift the flour, cocoa and baking powder over the yolk mixture and fold gently, then fold in the whites in three additions, followed by the melted butter.

  5. Divide between the tins and bake for 18 to 20 minutes until springy. Cool 10 minutes in the tins.

  6. Simmer 200 ml of the reserved cherry syrup with the sugar for 3 minutes, then take off the heat and stir in half the kirsch.

  7. Brush the warm sponges generously with this syrup and cool completely.

  8. Simmer the remaining cherry syrup with the cornflour until thickened, then fold through two-thirds of the cherries and the remaining kirsch. Cool.

  9. Whip the cold cream with the icing sugar and vanilla to firm peaks — stop before it turns grainy.

  10. Layer sponge, a ring of piped cream, cherry filling, sponge, and repeat. Cover the whole cake in the remaining cream, press chocolate curls onto the sides, and top with piped rosettes and whole cherries. Chill 4 hours before slicing.

Worth Knowing Before You Cook

Use sour Morello cherries, not sweet ones — the acidity is what balances the cream and chocolate.

Pipe a dam of cream around each layer before adding the cherry filling, or it will squeeze out.

Make chocolate curls by dragging a vegetable peeler along the side of a room-temperature bar.

Leftovers

Refrigerate 3 days. It is best on the second day once the syrup has settled. Not suitable for freezing once assembled.

Black Forest Cake: Your Questions Answered

How long does Black Forest Cake take to make?

45 minutes of preparation and 35 minutes of cooking, plus 4 hours of chilling, so allow 5 hours 20 minutes from start to finish. The method also needs time for cooling completely, which it does not time, so treat that as a minimum. The recipe serves 12.
