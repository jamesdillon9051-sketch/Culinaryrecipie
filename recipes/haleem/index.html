Haleem

Wheat, barley, lentils and meat cooked for hours and then pounded together until the whole pot becomes one smooth, stringy mass.

Why This Recipe Works

Haleem is judged on whether the meat has disappeared into the grain — you should not be able to find a piece of it, only strands. That only happens with sustained pounding while the pot is hot, which develops the wheat starch and shreds the meat at the same time. Cooking the pulses and the meat separately first means neither is overdone by the time the other is ready.

Method

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Drain the soaked wheat and barley, add all the dals, cover with 1.5 litres of water and simmer 90 minutes until everything has completely collapsed. Top up with hot water as needed.

  2. Meanwhile, heat the ghee in a separate heavy pot and fry the onions for 15 minutes until deep brown. Lift half out for garnish.

  3. Add the ginger-garlic paste and cook 2 minutes, then the chilli, turmeric, cumin and coriander, and fry 2 minutes.

  4. Add the meat and brown it well, then add 1 litre of water and the salt. Simmer covered for 2 hours until it falls apart.

  5. Blend the grain and lentil mixture with a stick blender until smooth.

  6. Shred the meat in its own pot with two forks, then combine everything into the larger pot.

  7. Now pound and beat the mixture with a wooden masher or the flat of a heavy spoon for 20 to 30 minutes over very low heat, until it becomes uniform, thick and slightly stringy. Add hot water if it gets too stiff.

  8. Stir in the garam masala and taste for salt.

  9. Serve topped with fried onions, ginger, chilli and coriander, with lemon and chaat masala on the side.

Chef’s Tips

  • Pound it for at least twenty minutes. That is the entire dish.
  • Cook the grains and the meat separately, then combine.
  • Keep hot water to hand — haleem thickens fast and catches easily.

Pairing Suggestions

  • Naan
  • Lemon, ginger and chaat masala
  • Sweet lassi

Storage & Reheating

Refrigerate up to 4 days and freeze 3 months. Reheat with water, beating as it warms.
