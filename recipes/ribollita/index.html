Ribollita

Tuscan bread soup: cannellini beans, cavolo nero and soffritto cooked down, thickened with stale bread and then reboiled the next day.

★★★★★★★★★★4.7(1,422)Rated 4.7 out of 5 from 1422 reviews 1 hr 25 min total Serves 6 servings Easy

Why This Recipe Works

The name means "reboiled", and it is not romanticism: the soup is made one day, left overnight for the bread to dissolve completely into it, and reheated the next, which is when it becomes a single thick mass rather than soup with bread in it. Puréeing a third of the beans gives body without cream or stock.

Method

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Simmer the soaked beans with the sage and whole garlic in unsalted water for 60 minutes until tender. Salt them only at the end, and keep all the cooking liquid.

  2. Blend a third of the beans with a ladle of their liquid until smooth. Leave the rest whole.

  3. Heat the olive oil in a very large pot and cook the onion, carrot and celery over low heat for 20 minutes, until completely soft and sweet. This soffritto is the whole base.

  4. Add the sliced garlic and chilli flakes for 1 minute, then the tomato purée for 2 minutes.

  5. Add the tinned tomatoes and cook 10 minutes.

  6. Add the cavolo nero, cabbage, potatoes, rosemary, the whole beans, the bean purée and about 1.2 litres of the bean cooking liquid.

  7. Simmer, half covered, for 45 minutes, until the greens have collapsed and the soup has darkened. Season.

  8. Stir in the torn bread, take the pot off the heat, and leave it to stand at room temperature for at least an hour — ideally overnight in a cool place.

  9. The next day, reheat slowly, stirring, until the bread has completely disappeared into the soup and it is thick enough for a spoon to stand up in.

  10. Serve in warm bowls with a heavy pour of raw olive oil over each.

Chef’s Tips

  • Make it a day ahead. Reboiled is the recipe, not a suggestion.
  • Twenty minutes on the soffritto. Everything else depends on it.
  • Finish with far more raw olive oil than seems polite.

Pairing Suggestions

  • A Tuscan red such as Chianti
  • More olive oil
  • Grated Parmesan, if not keeping it vegan

Storage & Reheating

Refrigerate up to 4 days, improving daily, and freeze 3 months.
