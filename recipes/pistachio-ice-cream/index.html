Pistachio Ice Cream

A custard ice cream made with ground pistachios and a hint of almond, chilled for 4 hours, churned and frozen for 4 more.

45 min prep and cooking plus 8 hr 30 min Steeping, chilling and freezing 9 hr 15 min start to finish Serves 8 servings Medium

Cooking Pistachio Ice Cream

Before you begin

  • Plan for 8 hr 30 min of Steeping, chilling and freezing. It is part of the 9 hr 15 min start to finish.

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Blanch the 150 g pistachios for 1 minute, drain and rub off the skins. Toast at 160°C / 320°F for 5 minutes. Grind to a fine paste with 2 tbsp of the sugar.

  2. Heat the cream and milk until steaming and whisk in the pistachio paste. Cover and leave for 30 minutes.

  3. Whisk the yolks, remaining sugar and salt until pale. Reheat the pistachio milk, whisk a ladle into the yolks and return to the pan. Cook to 80°C / 176°F, stirring.

  4. Strain, pressing hard, stir in the almond extract and chill for 4 hours.

  5. Churn until thick, stir in the chopped pistachios and freeze for 4 hours.

Why It Works

Real pistachio ice cream is a dull sage-brown, not the bright green of the shop-bought kind. The colour comes from the nuts themselves, and the flavour is nutty, sweet and faintly floral. Italians, especially in Sicily, take it seriously, and the best come from the town of Bronte, where the volcanic soil is said to give the pistachios their depth.

The nuts must be unsalted and raw. Blanch them in boiling water for 1 minute, drain, and slip the skins off by rubbing them in a tea towel, which leaves the pale green kernels underneath. Toast them lightly for 5 minutes at 160°C / 320°F, then grind them to a fine paste in a food processor with a spoonful of sugar. This paste is the flavour of the ice cream.

Add a drop of almond extract. A tiny amount, no more than a quarter teaspoon, boosts the nutty flavour and rounds it out, and is common in Italian recipes. More than that tastes of marzipan. Stir the paste into the warm custard while it is still hot, so the nut oils infuse the milk, and let the mixture steep for 30 minutes.

Strain through a fine sieve, pressing hard, and chill for 4 hours. After churning, stir in a handful of roughly chopped pistachios for crunch. Freeze for 4 hours until firm.

Storage and Reheating

Keeps in the freezer for 2 weeks.
