Lemon Delicious Pudding

A light lemon sponge that bakes with its own tangy custard sauce underneath. Beaten egg whites float to the top and the sauce sinks.

Lemon delicious is an Australian pudding that divides itself as it bakes: a light lemon sponge on top and a thin, tangy lemon custard underneath. The batter is a curdled-looking mixture of butter, sugar, egg yolks, flour, lemon juice and milk, and the egg whites are whisked separately and folded in. The whisked whites are what make the sponge: they hold air, and the air floats up to form a light cake on top, while the heavier liquid sinks to form the sauce. The dish is set in a bath of hot water in the oven, so that the custard cooks gently and does not curdle.

What People Ask

How long does Lemon Delicious Pudding take to make?

About 1 hour 10 minutes in total — 25 minutes of preparation and 45 minutes of cooking. The recipe serves 6.

What are the key tips for making Lemon Delicious Pudding?

Whisk the whites to soft peaks, no further. Stiff whites are hard to fold in without deflating them. Use a clean, dry bowl for the whites. Any grease stops them whipping. Do not skip the water bath. It cooks the custard gently and stops it splitting.

What should I serve with Lemon Delicious Pudding?

Vanilla ice cream; Pouring cream; Fresh berries.

The Method for Lemon Delicious Pudding

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C / 350°F. Butter a 1.5-litre baking dish and boil the kettle.

  2. Beat the butter, lemon zest and sugar until pale and creamy. Beat in the egg yolks one at a time.

  3. Stir in the flour, then the lemon juice and milk. The mixture will look curdled. This is normal.

  4. Whisk the egg whites to soft peaks in a clean bowl. Fold them gently into the lemon mixture in two additions.

  5. Pour into the dish and set it in a deep roasting tin. Pour boiling water into the tin to come halfway up the sides of the dish.

  6. Bake for 40 minutes, until the top is golden and springy.

  7. Serve at once, scooping down to the custard underneath.

Tips That Matter

  • Whisk the whites to soft peaks, no further. Stiff whites are hard to fold in without deflating them.
  • Use a clean, dry bowl for the whites. Any grease stops them whipping.
  • Do not skip the water bath. It cooks the custard gently and stops it splitting.

Ingredient Swaps

  • No self-raising flour to hand? Use the same weight of plain flour plus 2 teaspoons of baking powder per 150 g.

Serve It With

Vanilla ice cream · Pouring cream · Fresh berries

Leftovers and Storage

Best eaten at once. Leftovers keep covered in the fridge for 2 days and are good cold.
