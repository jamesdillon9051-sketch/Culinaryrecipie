Bumbleberry Pie

A double-crust pie of rhubarb, apple and mixed berries. Bumbleberry is not a fruit but a Canadian summer habit: sharp, sweet and deep purple.

1 hr 40 min prep and cooking plus 3 hr 30 min Chilling and cooling 5 hr 10 min start to finish Serves 8 servings Medium

The Method for Bumbleberry Pie

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Make the pastry: mix the flour, salt and sugar, rub in the butter until it looks like coarse crumbs, then add the ice water a tablespoon at a time until the dough just comes together. Divide into two discs, one slightly larger, then wrap and chill for at least 30 minutes.

  2. Heat the oven to 220°C / 425°F with a baking tray on the lower shelf.

  3. Roll the larger disc to 3 mm thick and line a 23 cm pie dish, leaving the edge overhanging.

  4. Toss the rhubarb, apple, strawberries, blueberries and raspberries gently with the sugar, cornflour, lemon juice and cinnamon. Pile into the pastry shell.

  5. Roll out the second disc, lay it over the fruit, trim and crimp the edges to seal. Cut 5 slits in the top, brush with the milk and sprinkle with the sugar.

  6. Set the pie on the hot tray and bake for 15 minutes. Lower the oven to 180°C / 350°F and bake for 40 to 45 minutes more, until the crust is deep golden and the juices bubble through the slits. Cover the edge with foil if it browns too fast.

  7. Cool on a wire rack for at least 3 hours before slicing.

Things to Watch For

  1. Cut the rhubarb small, about 1 cm. Large pieces stay hard while the berries collapse.
  2. The hot tray gives a crisp base. It also catches any juice that bubbles over.
  3. Frozen berries work if you do not thaw them first. Toss them in the cornflour while still frozen.

About This Recipe

Bumbleberry is not a berry. It is a name for a pie of mixed fruit, usually rhubarb and apple with whichever berries are ripe, and it is a Canadian summer habit, especially in the West. The mixture is balanced on purpose: rhubarb and apple keep their shape and bring sharpness, and the soft berries bring colour and sweetness. Cornflour thickens the juice so it stays in the pie, and the pie must cool for three hours, because a pie cut hot runs. The pastry is a plain all-butter one, rolled to 3 mm, and the pie goes in a hot oven first to set the base.

What to Serve It With

Vanilla ice cream · Custard · Whipped cream

Leftovers

Keep loosely covered at room temperature 1 day, then in the fridge up to 4 days. Freeze whole, baked, for 2 months.

Quick Answers

How long does Bumbleberry Pie take to make?

40 minutes of preparation and 1 hour of cooking, plus 3 hours 30 minutes of Chilling and cooling, so allow 5 hours 10 minutes from start to finish. The recipe serves 8.
