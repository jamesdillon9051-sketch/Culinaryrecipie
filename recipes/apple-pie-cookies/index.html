The Case for Apple Pie Cookies

Cook the apples first. That is the single most useful instruction, because raw apple releases water as it bakes and turns the cookie soggy from the inside.

Simmer the diced apple with the sugar, cinnamon and lemon juice for 8 minutes, until soft and syrupy, then stir in a spoonful of cornflour to thicken it. Cool the filling completely. Warm filling melts the dough as you shape it. Keep the filling thick. A runny one leaks through the seams and burns on the tray.

Make the dough from butter, sugar, egg, flour and a pinch of cinnamon. It should be soft but not sticky. Flatten a ball of dough in your palm, put a teaspoon of filling in the middle and fold the dough up around it, sealing the seam well.

Bake at 180°C for 15 minutes, until golden at the edges. If your oven runs hot, check at 12 minutes. Cool on the tray for 5 minutes.

How to Cook Apple Pie Cookies

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Simmer the apples with the caster sugar, cinnamon and lemon juice for 8 minutes until soft. Stir in the cornflour for 1 minute and cool completely.

  2. Heat the oven to 180°C / 355°F and line two trays. Beat the butter and brown sugar until pale, then beat in the egg and vanilla.

  3. Mix in the flour, baking powder, cinnamon and salt to a soft dough.

  4. Flatten a walnut-sized ball of dough in your palm, put a teaspoon of filling in the middle, fold the dough round it and seal. Make twelve.

  5. Bake for 15 minutes until golden at the edges. Cool on the tray for 5 minutes.
