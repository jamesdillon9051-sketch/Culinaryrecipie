Bobotie

Curried minced beef with dried fruit under a set savoury custard, baked with bay leaves standing in the top. South Africa's national dish, and Cape Malay in origin.

Why This Recipe Works

Bread soaked in milk and squeezed keeps the mince moist and gives the filling a soft, almost pâté-like texture rather than a granular one. The custard must go onto a cooled filling or it curdles from below before it has a chance to set into a smooth layer.

Method

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Soak the bread in the 150 ml of milk for 10 minutes, then squeeze it out, reserving the milk, and crumble it.

  2. Fry the onions in the oil for 10 minutes until soft and golden, then add the garlic for 1 minute.

  3. Add the curry powder, turmeric, coriander and cloves and fry 90 seconds until fragrant.

  4. Raise the heat, add the mince, and brown well for 10 minutes, breaking it up.

  5. Stir in the soaked bread, apricots, raisins, both chutneys, vinegar, almonds and salt.

  6. Add the reserved soaking milk and simmer 10 minutes until thick and no longer wet.

  7. Taste and adjust — it should be gently sweet, tangy and fragrant rather than hot.

  8. Spread into a 25 cm baking dish, level the surface, and cool for 20 minutes.

  9. Whisk the custard eggs, milk, salt and turmeric and pour over the cooled filling. Push the bay leaves in upright so they stand proud.

  10. Bake at 180°C / 350°F for 35 to 40 minutes until the custard is set and golden. Rest 10 minutes before serving.

Chef’s Tips

  • Cool the filling before the custard goes on, or it will curdle rather than set.
  • Mild curry powder is correct. Bobotie is aromatic and fruity, not fiery.
  • The bay leaves are traditional and perfume the custard as it bakes; remove them before eating.

Pairing Suggestions

  • Yellow rice with raisins
  • Sliced banana, coconut and chutney as sambals
  • A South African Pinotage or Chenin Blanc

Storage & Reheating

Refrigerate up to 3 days and freeze 2 months. Reheat covered at 170°C / 340°F for 25 minutes.
