Why Ciorba Radauteana Works

The first sign it is ready is the colour: a pale golden broth, thickened with cream and egg yolks, and a strong smell of garlic. Ciorbă rădăuțeană is the chicken soup of the Rădăuți region in northern Romania, and it differs from the plain chicken soup in its creamy, sharp finish: garlic and vinegar are stirred in at the end.

Simmer chicken pieces in water with an onion, carrot, celery and parsnip for 40 minutes, until tender. Lift out the chicken, shred it and strain the broth. Return the broth to the pot with diced vegetables from the stock, or a few fresh ones. Whisk egg yolks with soured cream in a bowl and add a ladle of hot broth a little at a time. Pour the mixture into the soup off the heat, stirring, so that it thickens without curdling.

Crush garlic to a paste with salt and stir into the soup with a splash of white vinegar. Return the shredded chicken. The soup should be creamy, tangy and sharp with garlic. Serve at once in deep bowls with chopped parsley and bread. Do not let it boil after the egg is added.

Making Ciorba Radauteana, Step by Step

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Simmer the chicken, onion, carrots, parsnip, celery and salt in the water for 40 minutes. Lift out the chicken and vegetables.

  2. Strain the broth. Shred the chicken, discarding skin and bones, and dice the carrots and parsnip.

  3. Whisk the yolks and soured cream with a ladle of hot broth. Pour into the broth off the heat, stirring.

  4. Stir in the garlic and vinegar. Return the chicken and diced vegetables.

  5. Serve with the parsley.

A Few Notes

Add the egg and cream off the heat.

Do not boil the soup after that.

Add the garlic and vinegar last.

Ingredient Swaps

  • An eighth of a teaspoon of garlic powder per clove is the usual stand-in when you are out of fresh garlic — it will not caramelise the same way, but it carries the flavour.
  • A dry vermouth keeps for longer than an open bottle of white wine and is the closer swap than a random white left in the fridge; for an alcohol-free version, use the same amount of stock with a squeeze of lemon.

Leftovers

Keeps for 2 days in the fridge. Reheat very gently.
