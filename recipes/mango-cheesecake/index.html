Mango Cheesecake

A no-bake cheesecake of cream cheese, mango purée and whipped cream on a buttery biscuit base, set with gelatine and topped with fresh mango and passionfruit.

35 min prep and cooking plus 6 hr 30 min Chilling 7 hr 5 min start to finish Serves 10 servings Medium

Why This Is Worth Making

Mangoes are one of the fruits of the Australian summer, and when they are in season, from November to March, they are eaten in every form, by the cheek, in salads, in smoothies and, on the Christmas table, in a mango cheesecake. It is a cake that suits the climate, since it needs no oven and is served cold, and it is a good way to use the fruit, whose sweet, perfumed flesh purées to a smooth, bright golden cream that gives the filling its flavour and its colour. The structure is that of a classic no-bake cheesecake: a base of crushed biscuits held with butter, and a filling of cream cheese beaten with sugar, into which the mango purée is stirred and whipped cream is folded, with gelatine to set it. Unlike kiwifruit, pineapple or papaya, mango does not contain an enzyme that breaks down gelatine, so the fresh purée can be used without cooking. The cheesecake needs several hours to set firmly enough to cut. Slices of fresh mango and the pulp of a passionfruit on top add a sharp, tropical contrast to the sweetness.

How to Make Mango Cheesecake

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Grease and line a 23 cm springform tin. Mix the crushed biscuits with the melted butter, press firmly into the base of the tin, and refrigerate for 30 minutes.

  2. Sprinkle the gelatine over the cold water in a small bowl and leave for 5 minutes to swell, then warm it in the microwave for 15 to 20 seconds, until clear. Let it cool a little.

  3. Beat the cream cheese and sugar together in a large bowl until smooth, then beat in the mango purée. Stir in the gelatine.

  4. Whip the cream to soft peaks and fold it in in two additions until smooth.

  5. Pour the filling over the base, smooth the top, and refrigerate for at least 6 hours, until firm.

  6. Arrange the mango slices on top and spoon over the passionfruit pulp.

  7. Run a knife around the edge, release the springform, and serve chilled.

Notes From the Method

Use very ripe, fragrant mangoes. They give a sweet, deep flavour and a smooth purée, and underripe ones are sour and fibrous.

Cool the melted gelatine before you add it. Hot gelatine can split the cream cheese, and cold gelatine sets in strings.

Give the cheesecake the full time to set. It needs several hours before it holds a clean slice, and it is best made the day before.

Storing and Reheating

Keeps covered in the fridge for up to 3 days. Freeze the un-topped cheesecake for up to 2 months and thaw in the fridge overnight, then top with fresh mango.
