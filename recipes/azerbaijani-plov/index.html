Why This Azerbaijani Plov Recipe Works

Three dishes make one meal: the rice, the garnish, and the crust. In Azerbaijan, plov is not mixed together. The saffron rice sits in one dish, the stewed lamb with dried fruit (called gara) in another, and the crisp lavash lining the pan is served on the side as the prize.

The rice is basmati, soaked in salted water, then boiled briefly until it is nearly cooked and drained. Drain it while it still has a firm centre. It finishes cooking in steam, and soft rice will turn to paste in the pot.

To build the crust, line a heavy pot with a thin sheet of lavash, brush it with melted butter and saffron water, then pile the rice in a cone on top and make a few holes with a spoon handle for steam. Cover with a cloth wrapped in the lid, and cook over a low heat for 40 minutes. The bottom turns golden and crisp.

The gara is made by browning lamb with onions and simmering it with dried apricots, prunes and chestnuts and a little cinnamon until tender, about 45 minutes. Serve the rice with the crust on top, the gara beside it.

Making Azerbaijani Plov

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Soak the rice in cold water with the 2 tbsp salt for 1 hour, then drain. Boil in plenty of water for 6 minutes until nearly tender, then drain.

  2. For the gara, melt the butter and brown the lamb for 6 minutes. Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes. Add the dried fruit, chestnuts, cinnamon, turmeric, salt and water. Cover and simmer for 45 minutes.

  3. Soak the saffron in the hot water. Line a heavy pot with the lavash and brush with some of the melted butter and saffron water.

  4. Pile the rice in a cone on the lavash, make holes with a spoon handle and pour over the remaining butter and saffron water. Wrap the lid in a clean cloth and cover tightly. Cook over a low heat for 40 minutes.

  5. Turn out or spoon out the rice, break the crust into pieces and serve with the gara.

Small Things That Help

  • Wrap the lid in a cloth to catch condensation.
  • Do not stir the rice once it is in the pot.
  • Use a non-stick pot for an easy release.

Azerbaijani Plov: Questions and Answers

How long does Azerbaijani Plov take to make?

About 2 hours of hands-on work — 30 minutes of preparation and 1 hour 30 minutes of cooking — plus 1 hour of Soaking the rice, so allow 3 hours from start to finish. The recipe serves 6.

What are the key tips for making Azerbaijani Plov?

Wrap the lid in a cloth to catch condensation. Do not stir the rice once it is in the pot. Use a non-stick pot for an easy release.

What should I serve with Azerbaijani Plov?

Fresh herbs; Yoghurt; Pickled vegetables; Tea.

How should Azerbaijani Plov be stored?

Keeps in the fridge for 2 days. Reheat with a splash of water.
