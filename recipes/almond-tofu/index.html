Almond Tofu

A set almond cream cut into cubes and floated in syrup with fruit. Not tofu and not really almond — it is apricot kernel, and it tastes of neither.

Why This Recipe Works

Agar has to be boiled, properly, for at least two minutes — unlike gelatine it will not set from a warm dissolve, and an agar jelly that has been heated but not boiled simply stays liquid in the fridge. It also sets at room temperature and sets firm, so pour it into the mould promptly and do not expect the soft wobble of a gelatine dessert. Use almond extract sparingly: a teaspoon in a litre is plenty, and more tastes of marzipan.

Method

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Whisk the agar powder into the almond milk while it is cold, so it disperses without lumps.

  2. Add the whole milk, sugar and salt and bring to the boil, whisking.

  3. Boil 2 full minutes, whisking constantly — agar must boil or it will not set.

  4. Take it off the heat, stir in the almond extract, and pour into a shallow dish through a sieve.

  5. Leave to set at room temperature 30 minutes, then refrigerate 2 hours until firm.

  6. Simmer the syrup water, sugar and lemon peel together 5 minutes, then chill it.

  7. Cut the set jelly into 2 cm cubes or diamonds with a wet knife.

  8. Divide between bowls, add the fruit, and pour the cold syrup over.

Chef’s Tips

  • Boil the agar for two full minutes. Warm is not enough.
  • It sets at room temperature, so pour it promptly.
  • A teaspoon of almond extract, no more.

Pairing Suggestions

  • Lychees
  • Green tea
  • A hot evening

Storage & Reheating

Refrigerated for 4 days, covered. Do not freeze — agar weeps when thawed.
