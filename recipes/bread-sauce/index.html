Bread Sauce

Milk infused with onion, clove and bay, then thickened with nothing but breadcrumbs. Older than gravy, better than it sounds, and the thing that makes a plate of roast turkey worth eating.

35 min prep and cooking plus 30 min infusing 1 hr 5 min start to finish Serves 8 servings Easy

Why Bread Sauce Works

The whole flavour comes from an infusion, so the milk is brought to a bare simmer with the aromatics and then left off the heat for half an hour — hot milk extracts, boiling milk catches and tastes scalded. Fresh breadcrumbs rather than dried is not fussiness: dried crumbs have already lost their starch structure and turn the sauce granular, while fresh ones swell and collapse into a texture closer to a very loose porridge. The butter and cream go in at the end, off the heat, for gloss.

How to Make Bread Sauce

Before you begin

  • Plan for 30 min of infusing. It is part of the 1 hr 5 min start to finish.
  • This is a sauce, not a meal on its own. The serving suggestions below say what it goes with.

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Stud each onion half with 4 cloves — pushing them through a bay leaf holds everything together and makes them easy to fish out.

  2. Put the onion, bay, peppercorns and mace in a pan with the milk and bring to a bare simmer over a low heat.

  3. Take off the heat the moment it steams, cover, and leave to infuse for 30 minutes. Do not let it boil at any point.

  4. Strain the milk into a clean pan, pressing the onion, and discard the aromatics.

  5. Stir in the breadcrumbs and set over a low heat for 8 to 10 minutes, stirring often, until thickened to a loose porridge. It thickens considerably as it stands.

  6. Take off the heat and beat in the butter, cream, salt, nutmeg and white pepper.

  7. Taste — it needs more salt than you expect — and serve warm rather than hot.

Notes for Getting It Right

  • Fresh breadcrumbs, from a day-old white loaf, blitzed. Packet crumbs make a gritty sauce.
  • Leave it looser than you want it. It sets firm within ten minutes of leaving the heat.
  • The infused milk can be made the day before and strained cold.

Common Substitutions & Variations

  • In a sauce, full-fat crème fraîche can usually take the place of the double cream; single cream is thinner and more likely to split if it boils, so keep it below a simmer.

Serving Suggestions

  • Roast turkey or goose
  • Cranberry sauce
  • Pigs in blankets

Keeping and Reheating

Refrigerated for 3 days. Reheat gently with a splash of milk, whisking, as it sets solid when cold.

Bread Sauce FAQ: Common Questions

How long does Bread Sauce take to make?

10 minutes of preparation and 25 minutes of cooking, plus 30 minutes of infusing, so allow 1 hour 5 minutes from start to finish. The recipe serves 8.
