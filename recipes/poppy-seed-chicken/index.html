Poppy Seed Chicken

Shredded chicken in a creamy sauce topped with buttery cracker crumbs and poppy seeds, cooked for 35 minutes.

Cooking Poppy Seed Chicken

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C / 355°F. Melt the 30 g butter, stir in the flour for 1 minute, then whisk in the milk and simmer for 3 minutes. Off the heat, stir in the soured cream, mustard, garlic powder, salt and pepper.

  2. Fold in the chicken and tip into a baking dish.

  3. Toss the crackers with the melted butter and poppy seeds and scatter over the top.

  4. Cook for 35 minutes until bubbling and golden.

Why the Method Works

It is a staple of church suppers in the American South: a creamy chicken filling under a crunchy, buttery topping. The topping is where the interest is, and it takes only crackers, butter and poppy seeds.

Cook the chicken first, either by poaching it for 15 minutes or by using leftover roast chicken, and shred it with two forks. Shred it coarsely, not to a paste, so that you can taste it.

The sauce is soured cream, a thick white sauce and a spoon of mustard, which stops it being heavy. Season well with salt, pepper and a pinch of garlic powder.

Crush the crackers and toss with melted butter and the seeds until every crumb is coated, or the topping will go pale. Spread it over the chicken. Cook at 180°C for 35 minutes, until the sauce bubbles at the edges and the top is golden. If your oven runs hot, check at 28 minutes.

After You've Cooked It

Keeps in the fridge for 3 days. Reheat in the oven to keep the topping crisp.

Adapting It for Your Diet

  • A measure-for-measure gluten-free flour blend can generally replace the plain flour here without changing the method, since this is savoury cooking rather than a structural bake.

What should I serve with Poppy Seed Chicken?

Keeps in the fridge for 3 days. Reheat in the oven to keep the topping crisp.
