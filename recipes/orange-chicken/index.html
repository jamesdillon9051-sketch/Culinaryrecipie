Orange Chicken

The most ordered dish in American Chinese restaurants: craggy fried chicken tossed in a bright, sticky sauce built on real orange juice and zest rather than marmalade. Crisp for as long as it takes to carry it to the table.

Why This Recipe Works

A cornflour-heavy batter fries into a rough, porous shell that grips sauce instead of shedding it, and frying twice drives out the moisture that would soften it from within. The sauce is thickened off the boil with a slurry so it stays glossy and clear rather than turning cloudy and gluey.

Method

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Toss the chicken with the egg white, soy sauce and Shaoxing wine and leave 20 minutes.

  2. Mix the cornflour, flour and baking powder. Lift the chicken out piece by piece and press it into the dry mix until thickly and unevenly coated. Those rough edges are what catch the sauce.

  3. Heat the oil to 170°C / 340°F and fry in three batches for 4 minutes each until pale gold. Drain on a rack.

  4. Raise the oil to 195°C / 385°F and fry each batch again for 90 seconds until deep gold and crackling. Drain.

  5. For the sauce, simmer the orange juice, zest strips, sugar, vinegar, soy, garlic, ginger and chilli in a wok for 5 minutes.

  6. Fish out the zest strips. Stir the cornflour slurry and pour it in, whisking, then simmer 60 seconds until the sauce is glossy and coats a spoon.

  7. Add the chicken and toss hard for 30 seconds — no longer, or the crust softens. Scatter with spring onion and sesame seeds and serve immediately.

Chef’s Tips

  • Zest in strips rather than gratings, so you can remove them. Grated zest turns the sauce bitter as it reduces.
  • Toss and serve. Orange chicken left sitting in its sauce is a different, sadder dish within five minutes.
  • Thighs stay juicy through a double fry where breast dries out. Use thighs.

Pairing Suggestions

  • Steamed jasmine rice
  • Stir-fried greens with garlic
  • Jasmine tea or a cold lager

Storage & Reheating

Refrigerate up to 3 days, keeping sauce and chicken separate if you can. Re-crisp the chicken at 200°C / 390°F for 8 minutes before saucing.
