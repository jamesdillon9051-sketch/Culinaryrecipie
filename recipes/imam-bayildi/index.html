Imam Bayildi

Aubergines split and stuffed with onion, garlic and tomato, then braised in a great deal of olive oil and served at room temperature. The name means "the imam fainted", and the traditional explanation involves the quantity of oil.

Why This Recipe Works

Salting the aubergines collapses their cell structure so they absorb less oil during frying, which sounds counterintuitive in a dish defined by olive oil but means the oil ends up in the sauce rather than making the flesh greasy. Serving at room temperature is not laziness: the olive oil flavour is far more expressive when it is not hot.

Method

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Peel the aubergines in stripes, leaving alternating bands of skin. Cut a deep slit lengthways down each without cutting through the ends.

  2. Salt them generously inside and out and leave in a colander for 45 minutes. Rinse and pat completely dry.

  3. Fry the whole aubergines in the 4 tablespoons of oil over medium heat for 10 minutes, turning, until browned and softened. Lift out.

  4. Add 100 ml of the olive oil to the pan and cook the onions over low heat for 20 minutes until completely soft and sweet.

  5. Add the garlic, green pepper and allspice for 3 minutes, then the chopped tomatoes, purée, sugar and salt.

  6. Cook 12 minutes until thick and jammy, then stir in the parsley and lemon juice off the heat.

  7. Set the aubergines in a baking dish, open the slits with two spoons, and stuff each generously with the filling.

  8. Lay tomato slices over the tops, pour the remaining 50 ml olive oil and the hot water around, and cover with foil.

  9. Bake at 180°C / 350°F for 50 minutes, uncovering for the last 15. Cool completely and serve at room temperature.

Chef’s Tips

  • Serve at room temperature. This is a zeytinyağlı dish, and hot is simply the wrong way to eat it.
  • Do not stint on the olive oil. It is the medium and the flavour, not an afterthought.
  • It is better on the second day, once the flavours have settled.

Pairing Suggestions

  • Warm flatbread
  • Thick yoghurt with garlic
  • A cold glass of ayran or a dry white

Storage & Reheating

Refrigerate up to 4 days. Bring fully to room temperature before serving — never eat it fridge-cold.
