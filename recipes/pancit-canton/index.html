Pancit Canton

Wheat noodles tossed with pork, prawns, chicken and vegetables in a soy and oyster sauce broth the noodles absorb as they cook.

Why This Recipe Works

The noodles finish cooking in the sauce rather than in water, absorbing the stock as it reduces, which is why the liquid is measured rather than poured to taste. Everything else is cooked and removed first so the vegetables stay crisp instead of stewing while the noodles soften.

Method

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Mix all the sauce ingredients in a jug.

  2. Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a wok over high heat and stir fry the prawns for 90 seconds. Remove.

  3. Add another tablespoon and stir fry the pork for 4 minutes and the chicken for 4 minutes, until browned. Remove.

  4. Add the last tablespoon of oil and fry the onion and garlic for 1 minute.

  5. Add the carrots and beans and stir fry 2 minutes, then the cabbage and pepper for 2 minutes. They should still be crisp. Remove everything to the plate with the meat.

  6. Pour the sauce into the wok and bring to a boil.

  7. Add the dry noodles, pushing them under, and cook 4 to 6 minutes, tossing constantly with tongs, until they have absorbed nearly all the liquid and are tender.

  8. Return the meat, prawns and vegetables and toss for 1 minute to combine and heat through.

  9. Taste and adjust with soy or pepper.

  10. Serve immediately with calamansi and spring onions.

Chef’s Tips

  • Cook everything separately and combine at the end. That is the whole method.
  • Noodles cook in the sauce, not in water.
  • Calamansi at the table. The dish is flat without the acid.

Pairing Suggestions

  • Lumpia spring rolls
  • Calamansi wedges
  • A cold beer

Storage & Reheating

Refrigerate up to 3 days. Reheat in a pan with a splash of stock; the noodles keep absorbing.
