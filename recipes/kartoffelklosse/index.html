Kartoffelklosse

Smooth potato dumplings of raw and cooked potato, simmered gently for 25 minutes and served with roast meat and gravy.

Why Kartoffelklosse Works

Keep the water below a boil. That is the rule for every dumpling, and it matters most here, because a rolling boil breaks these apart. Kartoffelklöße are the potato dumplings served with roast pork, goose or sauerbraten, and they should be light inside with a smooth skin.

Half the potatoes are boiled and mashed, and half are grated raw and squeezed dry in a cloth. Together they give a dumpling that holds its shape and is not heavy. Squeeze the grated potato very hard, since any water left makes the dough sticky and pushes you to add flour. Mix with a little potato starch, salt and nutmeg, and a beaten egg.

Shape into balls the size of a small apple with wet hands. Drop them into salted water that is just trembling, and simmer for 25 minutes. They rise when they are nearly ready. A toasted crouton pushed into the centre of each is the traditional touch. Lift out with a slotted spoon and serve at once with gravy.

How to Cook Kartoffelklosse

Before you begin

  • This is a side dish, not a meal on its own. The serving suggestions below say what it goes with.

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Boil half the potatoes in their skins for 25 minutes. Peel and mash while hot. Cool.

  2. Grate the raw potatoes and squeeze very dry in a cloth.

  3. Mix both with the starch, egg, salt and nutmeg to a firm dough.

  4. With wet hands, shape into 12 balls, pushing a few croutons into the middle of each.

  5. Slide into a pan of salted water just below a simmer and cook for 25 minutes. Lift out with a slotted spoon.

Worth Knowing Before You Cook

Squeeze the raw potato as dry as you can.

Keep the water below a boil.

Test one dumpling first and add starch if it breaks.

Leftovers

Keeps for 2 days in the fridge. Slice and fry the cold dumplings.
