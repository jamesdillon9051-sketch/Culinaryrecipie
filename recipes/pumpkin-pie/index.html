Classic Pumpkin Pie

A silky custard pie with proper spice and a blind-baked base that stays crisp. Cooking the purée down first concentrates it and eliminates the watery edge that spoils most versions.

★★★★★★★★★★4.8(2,688)Rated 4.8 out of 5 from 2688 reviews 1 hr 20 min total Serves 8 servings Easy

Why This Recipe Works

Tinned pumpkin carries a lot of water, and cooking it down with the spices before adding dairy both concentrates the flavour and blooms the fat-soluble spice compounds. A fully blind-baked, egg-sealed shell keeps the custard from soaking into the pastry during its long, slow bake.

Method

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Rub the butter into the flour, sugar and salt until it holds pea-sized pieces, add the ice water, and bring together. Chill 1 hour.

  2. Roll out and line a 23 cm pie dish, leaving an overhang. Crimp the edge and freeze for 30 minutes.

  3. Line with foil and baking beans and bake at 200°C / 390°F for 20 minutes. Remove the beans and bake 12 minutes more until the base is dry and sandy.

  4. Brush the hot shell with a little beaten egg and return to the oven for 2 minutes to seal it. Reduce the oven to 160°C / 320°F.

  5. Meanwhile, cook the pumpkin purée with both sugars, all the spices and the salt in a wide pan over medium heat for 6 to 8 minutes, stirring, until it darkens, thickens and begins to sputter.

  6. Take off the heat and whisk in the cream and milk, then cool for 5 minutes.

  7. Whisk in the eggs, yolk and bourbon, then strain through a sieve for a perfectly smooth custard.

  8. Pour into the warm shell and bake for 45 to 55 minutes, until the outer edge is set and the central 8 cm wobbles as one piece.

  9. Cool completely on a rack — at least 3 hours — then refrigerate 2 hours before serving with whipped cream.

Chef’s Tips

  • Do not overbake. A cracked pumpkin pie has gone past its setting point and turns grainy.
  • Pour the filling into a warm shell — cold filling in a cold shell takes far longer and soaks in.
  • Use pumpkin purée, not pumpkin pie filling, which is already sweetened and spiced.

Pairing Suggestions

  • Coffee, or a glass of bourbon
  • Serve with lightly whipped cream
  • The traditional close to Thanksgiving dinner

Storage & Reheating

Refrigerate 3 days. Freeze up to 1 month, though the crust softens slightly. Serve at cool room temperature.
