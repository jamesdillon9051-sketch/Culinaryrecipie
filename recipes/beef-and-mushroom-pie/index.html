The Idea Behind Beef and Mushroom Pie

Quick tip: let the filling go cold before the pastry goes on. A warm filling melts the butter in puff pastry from underneath, and the lid slumps instead of rising.

Brown the beef in batches, then soften the onion and mushrooms in the same pan. Stir in the flour, then the stock, tomato puree and Worcestershire sauce, and simmer for 90 minutes with the lid on. Stir in the mushrooms for the last 30 minutes. They release their juice and add flavour, but go to rags if cooked for the full time.

The gravy should be thick enough to coat a spoon, since the pastry will not soak up any liquid. If it is thin, simmer uncovered for 10 minutes. Cool completely, ideally overnight.

Fill a pie dish with the beef, cover with the pastry and trim the edge. Brush with egg and cut a steam hole. Bake at 200°C for 40 minutes, until deep gold. If your oven runs hot, check at 32 minutes. Rest for 10 minutes before cutting.

Making Beef and Mushroom Pie

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Brown the beef in the oil in batches and set aside. Soften the onion in the pan for 5 minutes.

  2. Stir in the flour, then the stock, tomato puree, Worcestershire sauce, thyme, salt and beef. Cover and simmer for 60 minutes.

  3. Add the mushrooms and simmer for 30 minutes more, uncovered for the last 10 if the gravy is thin. Cool completely.

  4. Heat the oven to 200°C / 390°F. Fill a 1.5 litre pie dish, cover with the pastry, trim, brush with egg and cut a steam hole.

  5. Bake for 40 minutes until deep gold. Rest for 10 minutes.

Notes for Getting It Right

  • Cool the filling before topping.
  • Add the mushrooms late.
  • If your oven runs hot, check at 32 minutes.
  • Thicken the gravy well.

Adapting It for Your Diet

  • A measure-for-measure gluten-free flour blend can generally replace the plain flour here without changing the method, since this is savoury cooking rather than a structural bake.
  • For a vegetarian version, look for a plant-based Worcestershire sauce — the anchovies in the standard bottle are what makes ordinary worcestershire sauce unsuitable, not anything else in it.

What to Serve with Beef and Mushroom Pie

  • Mashed potato
  • Peas
  • Gravy
  • Brown ale

Keeping and Reheating

Keeps in the fridge for 3 days. Reheat in a 180°C oven for 25 minutes.
