Cobb Salad

A composed salad from the Brown Derby in Hollywood: neat rows of chicken, bacon, avocado, egg, tomato and blue cheese over chopped leaves, with a sharp red wine vinaigrette. A genuinely satisfying main-course salad.

Why This Recipe Works

Arranging the ingredients in rows rather than tossing them lets each element keep its own texture and lets the eater control the ratio in every forkful. Everything is chopped to a similar small dice so a single fork can pick up all seven components at once, which was Robert Cobb's original point.

Method

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Lower the eggs into boiling water and cook for 9 minutes for a firm but not chalky yolk. Cool in iced water, peel and chop.

  2. Cook the bacon in a dry pan over medium heat until crisp, 8 to 10 minutes, then drain and chop.

  3. Whisk or shake the vinaigrette ingredients together until emulsified.

  4. Toss the chopped romaine and watercress with 3 tablespoons of the vinaigrette and spread across a large, wide platter.

  5. Arrange the chicken, bacon, egg, avocado, tomato and blue cheese in neat parallel rows across the leaves.

  6. Scatter the spring onions over the top and grind on plenty of black pepper.

  7. Bring the remaining vinaigrette to the table and let everyone dress and toss their own portion.

Chef’s Tips

  • Dice everything to roughly the same size — about 1 cm — so a single forkful carries all of it.
  • Toss the avocado in a little of the vinaigrette immediately to stop it browning.
  • Poached chicken is more tender than grilled here, and it soaks up the dressing better.

Pairing Suggestions

  • A dry rosé or a Chardonnay
  • Serve with crusty bread
  • Follow with fresh berries

Storage & Reheating

Components keep separately for 2 days. Assemble and dress at the last minute; a dressed Cobb wilts within 30 minutes.
