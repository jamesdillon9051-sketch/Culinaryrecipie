Bacalhau com Natas

Flaked salt cod, golden potatoes and soft onions baked under a creamy béchamel until bubbling. A Portuguese family classic.

Where This Goes Wrong

Taste a flake of the soaked cod before cooking. If it is still very salty, soak it for a few hours more.

Drain and dry the fried potatoes well. Wet potatoes make the bake watery.

Leave the salt out of the sauce until you have tasted it with the cod. It may not need any.

Cooking Bacalhau com Natas

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Soak the salt cod in a large bowl of cold water in the fridge for 24 hours, changing the water 3 times.

  2. Drain the cod, put it in a pan with fresh cold water, bring to a bare simmer and poach for 10 minutes, until it flakes. Drain, let cool for a few minutes, remove the skin and bones, and flake into large pieces.

  3. Heat the frying oil in a large frying pan over medium-high heat and fry the potato cubes in 2 batches for 10 to 12 minutes each, until golden and just tender. Drain on paper towels and sprinkle with a little salt.

  4. Heat the 3 tablespoons of olive oil in a second pan over medium heat and cook the onions and bay leaves for 15 minutes, until soft and golden. Add the garlic for 2 minutes, then discard the bay leaves.

  5. For the sauce, melt the butter in a saucepan, stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute. Whisk in the warm milk a little at a time, then the cream. Simmer for 5 minutes, stirring, until thick. Season with nutmeg and white pepper, and taste before adding salt, as the cod is salty.

  6. Heat the oven to 200°C / 400°F. Mix the cod, potatoes and onions in a large baking dish, spread them level and pour over the sauce. Scatter the cheese and olives on top.

  7. Bake for 20 to 25 minutes, until golden and bubbling. Scatter with parsley and rest for 5 minutes before serving.

Why the Method Works

Bacalhau com natas means salt cod with cream, and it is one of the best-loved of the very many ways the Portuguese cook dried salt cod, which is said to number one for every day of the year. Flaked cod is mixed with golden fried potatoes and soft, slowly cooked onions, covered with a thick, creamy white sauce, and baked until the top is golden and bubbling. It is a comforting, family dish, served for Sunday lunch and at celebrations, and it is simple to make once the cod has been soaked. The soaking is the one part that needs planning: dried cod is very salty and needs a full day in several changes of cold water before it can be cooked. Choose thick loin pieces, which flake into large, tender pieces.

Leftovers, Reheating and Keeping

Keep in the fridge up to 3 days and reheat in a 180°C oven for 20 minutes.

Common Substitutions & Variations

  • Double cream won't whip if you swap it, but for the cooking itself, full-fat crème fraîche or a lower-fat single cream works in its place.
  • An eighth of a teaspoon of garlic powder per clove is the usual stand-in when you are out of fresh garlic — it will not caramelise the same way, but it carries the flavour.

Quick Tips & Variations

  • A measure-for-measure gluten-free flour blend can generally replace the plain flour here without changing the method, since this is savoury cooking rather than a structural bake.

Questions and Answers

How long does Bacalhau com Natas take to make?

About 1 hour 50 minutes of hands-on work — 40 minutes of preparation and 1 hour 10 minutes of cooking — plus 1 day of Soaking the salt cod, so allow 1 day 2 hours from start to finish. The recipe serves 6.

What are the key tips for making Bacalhau com Natas?

Taste a flake of the soaked cod before cooking. If it is still very salty, soak it for a few hours more. Drain and dry the fried potatoes well. Wet potatoes make the bake watery. Leave the salt out of the sauce until you have tasted it with the cod. It may not need any.

What should I serve with Bacalhau com Natas?

A green salad; Vinho verde; Crusty bread.

Is Bacalhau com Natas suitable for a special diet?

This recipe is high-protein and no added sugar. Those labels are checked against the ingredient list, so a dish is only marked when nothing in it contradicts the claim.
