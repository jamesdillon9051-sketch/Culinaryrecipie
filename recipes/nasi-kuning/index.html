Why Nasi Kuning Works

At a birthday or a blessing in Indonesia, nasi kuning is moulded into a cone, a golden mountain topped with sliced omelette, fried shallots and chilli. The yellow colour stands for gratitude and good fortune, and the rice is eaten at the beginning of a celebration.

The flavour is built from coconut milk, fresh turmeric (or ground), lemongrass, pandan leaves and bay leaves, which flavour the rice as it cooks. Rinse the rice well, then soak it for 30 minutes before cooking. This helps the grains cook evenly and absorb the coconut milk without clumping. Use the full measure of liquid, and use a thick coconut milk. The grains swell and need the fat.

Bring the rice, coconut milk, water, turmeric, aromatics and salt to a boil, stir once, cover and cook on the lowest heat for 15 minutes. Then rest for 10 minutes without opening the lid. The rice should be soft, separate and a little glossy.

To serve in the traditional way, press the rice into a cone mould or a bowl and turn it out onto a plate, then decorate with strips of omelette, cucumber and fried shallots.

Making Nasi Kuning, Step by Step

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Drain the rice. Put it in a pan with the coconut milk, water, turmeric, lemongrass, pandan, bay leaves and salt. Bring to a boil, stir once, cover and cook on the lowest heat for 15 minutes.

  2. Rest, covered, for 10 minutes. Remove the aromatics and fluff with a fork.

  3. Heat the oil and make a thin omelette with the eggs. Roll up and slice into ribbons.

  4. Press the rice into a bowl, turn out onto a plate and decorate with the omelette, shallots and cucumber.

Common Substitutions & Variations

  • For a non-vegan version, double cream can generally stand in for the coconut milk; going the other way, a plant-based cream sold for cooking is the closer match to dairy than tinned coconut milk on its own.

Storing and Reheating

Keeps in the fridge for 2 days. Reheat with a splash of water.

About 50 minutes in total — 15 minutes of preparation and 35 minutes of cooking. The recipe serves 6.

What are the key tips for making Nasi Kuning?

Use thick coconut milk for a richer result. Do not lift the lid while cooking. Fresh turmeric gives a more fragrant rice.

What should I serve with Nasi Kuning?

How should Nasi Kuning be stored?

Keeps in the fridge for 2 days. Reheat with a splash of water.
