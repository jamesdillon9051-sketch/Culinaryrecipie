Jamaican Jerk Chicken

Chicken marinated overnight in scotch bonnet, allspice and thyme, then cooked slowly over indirect heat until the skin is lacquered and the smoke has worked its way through.

Why This Recipe Works

Allspice — pimento — is the defining flavour, and both the berries and the wood are traditionally used, which is why smoke matters as much as the marinade. Cooking over indirect heat for 40 minutes before finishing hot lets the fat render and the spices penetrate without the sugars in the marinade burning.

Method

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Blend all the marinade ingredients into a thick, coarse paste. Wear gloves when handling the scotch bonnets and do not touch your face.

  2. Reserve 4 tablespoons of the marinade for basting, then rub the rest into the chicken, deep into every slash.

  3. Marinate, covered, in the fridge for at least 12 hours and up to 24.

  4. Take the chicken out an hour before cooking and scrape off the thickest clumps of marinade, which would burn.

  5. Set up a barbecue for indirect heat, with coals banked to one side, or heat an oven to 180°C / 355°F.

  6. Cook the chicken skin-side up on the cool side, lid closed, for 35 to 45 minutes. Add a handful of soaked wood chips to the coals if you have them.

  7. Baste with the reserved marinade twice during this time.

  8. Move the chicken over direct heat for the final 6 to 8 minutes, turning frequently, until the skin is charred, lacquered and blistered and the meat reads 78°C.

  9. Rest 10 minutes, chop into pieces with a cleaver as they do in Jamaica, and serve with lime, rice and peas, and mango salsa.

Chef’s Tips

  • Scotch bonnets carry a fruity flavour as well as serious heat. Deseed them for less fire without losing that.
  • Indirect first, direct last. Reversing the order gives you burnt skin and raw meat.
  • The marinade keeps for weeks in the fridge and is excellent on pork or prawns.

Pairing Suggestions

  • A cold Red Stripe or a rum punch
  • Serve with rice and peas, fried plantain and festival dumplings
  • Finish with fresh pineapple

Storage & Reheating

Refrigerate 4 days, freeze 3 months. Reheat at 190°C / 375°F for 12 minutes; it is also excellent cold.
