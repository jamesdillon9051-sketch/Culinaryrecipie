Loco Moco

Rice, a hamburger patty, brown gravy and a fried egg, stacked in that order. Hawaii's answer to a plate lunch, and best eaten with a spoon.

Why This Recipe Works

The gravy is built in the pan the patties cooked in, so all the browned fond ends up in it — made separately it tastes like packet gravy on a burger. The egg has to be fried in hot fat with a runny yolk, because the yolk running into the gravy and rice is the point of the assembly.

Method

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Mix the mince with the grated onion, soy, Worcestershire, salt and pepper, and shape into 4 patties slightly wider than you want them — they shrink.

  2. Press a dimple into the centre of each so they cook flat.

  3. Heat the oil in a heavy frying pan and cook the patties 3 to 4 minutes a side until well browned. Set aside on a warm plate.

  4. In the same pan, melt the butter and cook the onion and mushrooms for 8 minutes, scraping up the fond from the patties.

  5. Stir in the flour and cook 2 minutes.

  6. Add the stock gradually, whisking, then the soy, Worcestershire and pepper.

  7. Simmer 6 to 8 minutes until the gravy coats a spoon. Return the patties and any resting juices for 2 minutes.

  8. In a separate pan, fry the eggs in hot oil until the whites are crisp at the edges and the yolks still liquid.

  9. Spoon a mound of hot rice into each bowl, sit a patty on top, ladle gravy generously over both, and slide the fried egg on last.

  10. Scatter with spring onions and serve immediately.

Chef’s Tips

  • Build the gravy in the patty pan. That fond is the flavour.
  • Runny yolks, always. They finish the dish at the table.
  • Dimple the patties so they stay flat under the egg.

Pairing Suggestions

  • Macaroni salad
  • A cold lager
  • Hot sauce

Storage & Reheating

The gravy and patties keep 3 days refrigerated. Cook rice and eggs fresh.
