Brown Soda Bread

A dense, nutty loaf of wholemeal and plain flour raised with bicarbonate of soda and buttermilk, baked for 40 minutes at 200C.

Brown Soda Bread: Step by Step

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C / 390°F and dust a tray with flour.

  2. Mix the wholemeal flour, plain flour, bicarbonate of soda and salt in a large bowl.

  3. Make a well, pour in the buttermilk and mix quickly with a hand to a soft, sticky dough.

  4. Tip onto a floured surface, shape into a round about 4 cm thick and place on the tray. Cut a deep cross.

  5. Bake for 40 minutes until the base sounds hollow when tapped. Wrap in a clean tea towel to cool.

What Can Go Wrong

  1. Bake as soon as the dough is mixed.
  2. Handle the dough as little as possible.
  3. Wrap in a cloth for a softer crust.

Common Substitutions & Variations

  • Out of buttermilk? The usual stand-in is a tablespoon of lemon juice or vinegar stirred into 250 ml of ordinary milk and left five minutes; it is thinner than cultured buttermilk, so a batter may need a little less of it.

The Short Version

The first sign it is ready is the sound: tap the base and it rings hollow, like a drum. Before that, the loaf will have risen into a rough dome, split along the cross cut on top, and turned a deep, nutty brown.

Soda bread needs no yeast and no proving. Bicarbonate of soda reacts with the acid in buttermilk the moment they meet, and the gas that forms lifts the loaf, so the dough must go into the oven straight away. Mix with a light hand, only until the flour is damp; overworking makes the bread tough and heavy. The dough will be sticky and shaggy, and that is right.

Shape it quickly into a round about 4 cm thick, cut a deep cross to let heat reach the middle, and bake. The crust is hard when it comes out, so wrap the loaf in a clean tea towel as it cools for a softer finish. Eat it the day it is made, with butter, or toast it the next day.

Keeping Brown Soda Bread

Best on the day. Keeps for 2 days wrapped; toast afterwards.
