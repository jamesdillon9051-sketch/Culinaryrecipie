Provençal Ratatouille

The Provençal original, in which each vegetable is cooked separately before they are married — the only way to get aubergine that is silky, courgette that still has bite and peppers that taste of themselves.

Why This Recipe Works

Aubergine, courgette and pepper release water and soften at wildly different rates; cooked together they turn into a uniform mush. Searing each one separately drives off moisture and builds browning before they are combined, so the final dish is a stew of distinct vegetables rather than a purée.

Method

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Salt the aubergine cubes and leave in a colander for 20 minutes, then pat dry. This collapses the cell structure so they absorb less oil.

  2. Heat 3 tablespoons of oil in a wide pan over medium-high and sear the aubergine for 8 minutes until browned and tender. Remove.

  3. Add 2 more tablespoons of oil and sear the courgette for 4 minutes until spotted but still firm. Remove.

  4. Sear the peppers for 6 minutes until blistered at the edges. Remove.

  5. Lower the heat, add the remaining oil and cook the onion for 10 minutes until soft and sweet, then the garlic for 1 minute.

  6. Stir in the tomato purée, cook 2 minutes, then add the tomatoes, thyme, bay and salt. Simmer for 15 minutes until it becomes a loose sauce.

  7. Fold all the vegetables back in and cook gently, uncovered, for 10 minutes so the flavours marry without further softening.

  8. Off the heat, stir in the vinegar and basil. Rest for at least 20 minutes — ratatouille is at its best warm, not hot, and better still the next day.

Chef’s Tips

  • Cut everything to a similar size so each spoonful carries all five vegetables.
  • The splash of vinegar at the end is what stops it tasting flat and sweet.
  • Serve with a poached egg on top for a complete supper.

Pairing Suggestions

  • A chilled Provence rosé is the obvious and correct answer
  • Serve with grilled lamb, roast chicken or simply crusty bread
  • Follow with fresh apricots and almond biscuits

Storage & Reheating

Improves over 3 to 4 days in the fridge. Freezes 3 months, though the courgette softens. Serve at room temperature or gently warmed.
