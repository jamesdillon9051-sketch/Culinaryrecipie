Ginisang Munggo

A Filipino stew of mung beans simmered with garlic, onion, tomato and spinach, flavoured with fish sauce.

Ginisang Munggo: Step by Step

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Heat the oil in a large pan and cook the onion and garlic for 4 minutes until soft.

  2. Add the tomatoes and cook for 3 minutes until they collapse.

  3. Add the mung beans and water and bring to the boil. Simmer for 30 minutes until the beans are soft and the stew is thick.

  4. Stir in the fish sauce and pepper, then the spinach until it wilts. Add water if the stew is too thick.

Keep an Eye on These

  1. Cook the onion, garlic and tomato until soft before the beans go in.
  2. Press a bean to check it is soft before seasoning.
  3. Add the fish sauce a spoonful at a time.
  4. Loosen the stew with hot water if it thickens as it stands.

Common Substitutions & Variations

  • An eighth of a teaspoon of garlic powder per clove is the usual stand-in when you are out of fresh garlic — it will not caramelise the same way, but it carries the flavour.

Quick Tips & Variations

  • No fish sauce, or keeping it vegetarian? A mix of soy sauce and a squeeze of lime gets close to the same salt-and-savoury lift the fish sauce gives.

Good to Know About Ginisang Munggo

The pot goes on early in the week, when money is tight and a bag of dried mung beans stretches a long way. Ginisang munggo is a staple of Filipino home cooking, and a dish that does not ask for much.

"Ginisa" refers to the base of garlic, onion and tomato cooked in oil, the start of countless Filipino dishes. Let the onion soften and the tomato collapse before the beans go in. Do not rush the base. It sets the flavour for the whole stew.

Mung beans need no soaking and cook in about 30 minutes. Test one by pressing it between your fingers. It should crush with no resistance, and some will have broken down to thicken the broth.

Spinach goes in at the end and wilts in the heat of the pot. Fish sauce adds the salty, savoury note; add it a little at a time and taste.

What to Serve It With

Steamed rice · Fried fish · Crispy fried pork · Lime wedges

Storing Leftovers

Keeps in the fridge for 4 days. It thickens as it cools, so add water when reheating.
