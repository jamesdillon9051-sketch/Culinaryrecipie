Lamb Hotpot

Lamb neck and onions in a thick gravy under a lid of overlapping sliced potatoes, baked slowly until the potatoes are crisp.

3 hr total Serves 6 servings Medium

How to Cook Lamb Hotpot

Before you begin

  • You will need: oven, large pot or casserole.

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Heat the oven to 160°C / 320°F. Brown the lamb in the oil in batches for 8 minutes each and set aside.

  2. Soften the onions in the same pan for 8 minutes, stir in the flour for 1 minute, then add the stock, Worcestershire sauce, bay leaves, salt and pepper and simmer for 10 minutes.

  3. Layer the lamb, carrots and gravy in a deep casserole.

  4. Overlap the potato slices in tight rings on top, brush with the melted butter and season.

  5. Cover and bake for 2 hours. Uncover, raise the oven to 200°C / 390°F and bake for 30 minutes until the potatoes are golden and crisp.

What Can Go Wrong

  1. Slice the potatoes thin and even.
  2. Overlap them tightly.
  3. If your oven runs hot, check the top at 20 minutes.
  4. Brush the potatoes with butter.

Adapting It for Your Diet

  • Where the plain flour thickens a sauce or coats something for frying, a gluten-free flour blend usually works in its place; check the blend's own instructions, and use stock and other packaged ingredients labelled gluten-free.

Good to Know About Lamb Hotpot

What separates a hotpot from a stew with potatoes on top? The potatoes. They are sliced thin, overlapped like roof tiles and brushed with butter, so that they crisp on top and steam in the gravy beneath.

Brown the lamb and soften the onions, then stir in the flour and pour on the stock with the Worcestershire sauce and bay leaves. Simmer for 10 minutes until it starts to thicken, then tip it into a deep casserole. Slice the potatoes thin and even, about 3 mm. Thick slices stay hard at the end.

Overlap the slices in tight rings, brush with the melted butter and season. Cover and bake at 160°C / 320°F for 2 hours, then uncover and bake at 200°C / 390°F for 30 minutes to brown the top. If your oven runs hot, check the top at 20 minutes.

The lamb should be soft and the potato lid crisp at the edges.

What to Serve It With

Pickled red cabbage · Buttered greens · Crusty bread · Mint sauce

Keeping Lamb Hotpot

Keeps in the fridge for 3 days. Reheat in a hot oven to crisp the potatoes.
