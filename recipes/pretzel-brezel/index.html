Bavarian Brezel

The Bavarian shape: fat belly, thin crossed arms, split along the base as it bakes, with coarse salt on the dark lye crust.

Why This Recipe Works

The pretzel bakers of Bavaria use food-grade lye, which is a stronger alkali than any home substitute; baking bicarbonate of soda in a low oven for an hour drives off carbon dioxide and gets much closer than the raw powder. Chilling the shaped pretzels uncovered dries the surface, and a dry surface is what takes the bath evenly and splits cleanly in the oven.

Method

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Spread the bicarbonate of soda on a lined tray and bake at 150C fan for 1 hour, then cool. It becomes a stronger alkali — keep it away from your eyes and aluminium pans.

  2. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar and salt. Add the water and oil and knead 10 minutes to a firm, smooth dough. It should feel stiffer than bread dough.

  3. Prove covered for 45 minutes — it will not double, and should not.

  4. Divide into 8. Roll each piece into a 55 cm rope that is fat in the middle and tapers to thin ends.

  5. Make a U, cross the ends twice, and press them onto the belly a third of the way up.

  6. Set the pretzels on lined trays and chill uncovered for 45 minutes. The surface should feel dry and leathery.

  7. Bring the 2 litres of water to a simmer, whisk in the baked soda, and dip each pretzel for 30 seconds, turning once.

  8. Drain, set back on the trays, scatter with coarse salt, and slash the fat belly horizontally with a sharp blade.

  9. Bake at 210C fan for 14 to 16 minutes until deep mahogany. Eat warm, the same day.

Chef’s Tips

  • Bake the bicarbonate of soda first, and handle it with gloves.
  • Chill uncovered until the surface is dry. Wet dough takes the bath badly.
  • Thin arms, fat belly. That contrast is the whole shape.

Pairing Suggestions

  • Obatzda or butter
  • Weisswurst and sweet mustard
  • A wheat beer

Storage & Reheating

Best within hours. Refresh at 180C fan for 4 minutes, or freeze baked for 1 month.
