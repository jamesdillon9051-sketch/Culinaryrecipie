Shrimp Fried Rice

Shrimp tossed with cold rice, egg whites, peas, carrot and spring onions, seasoned with one tablespoon of low-sodium soy sauce. Four servings in 25 minutes.

The Method for Shrimp Fried Rice

Before you begin

  • You will need: wok.

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Heat half the oil in a large wok over high heat. Stir-fry the carrot and peas for 3 minutes. Add the garlic, ginger and spring onions for 30 seconds.

  2. Add the rice, breaking up any lumps, and fry for 4 minutes until hot.

  3. Push the rice to the edge, add the remaining oil and shrimp to the centre and cook for 2 minutes, until pink. Pour in the egg whites and stir for 1 minute until just set.

  4. Mix everything together with the soy sauce, vinegar and pepper for 1 minute. Drizzle with the sesame oil.

What Can Go Wrong

  1. Use rice chilled overnight; fresh rice turns gluey in the pan.
  2. Cut large shrimp in half so that they cook through in 2 minutes.
  3. Keep the wok very hot; a cool pan steams the shrimp.

Changing the Recipe

  • Out of spring onions? A small regular onion, finely sliced, or a few chives stirred in at the end both cover the job.
  • If you only have ground ginger, a quarter-teaspoon for each tablespoon of fresh is the ratio usually given; it tastes warmer and less bright than the fresh root.
  • An eighth of a teaspoon of garlic powder per clove is the stand-in usually given when you are out of fresh garlic; it will not brown the same way, and it burns easily, so add it with the liquid.

Adapting It for Your Diet

  • For a gluten-free version, use a tamari or soy sauce labelled gluten-free in place of the soy sauce: ordinary soy sauce is brewed with wheat, and not every tamari is wheat-free.

The Short Version

A fried rice for the night when there is leftover rice and a bag of shrimp in the freezer. Shrimp cook so quickly that they go in last, after the rice has heated through and picked up some colour from the pan. The whole dish takes 25 minutes.

Cook the vegetables and rice first, so that the pan is hot and dry when the shrimp arrive. Shrimp need 2 minutes, no more: they turn pink, curl and are done. The egg whites go in with them and set around the rice in soft curds. Keep the pan hot.

Shrimp are sweet, so the dish needs less soy sauce than most fried rice. One tablespoon of the low-sodium kind is enough for the whole pan. Ginger, garlic and white pepper carry the rest. Use shrimp with no added phosphate, and thaw frozen ones in the fridge overnight, then pat them dry before they go in the pan.

Keeping Shrimp Fried Rice

Cool quickly and keep in the fridge for up to 1 day. Reheat until piping hot, in a hot pan, for no longer than 2 minutes.
