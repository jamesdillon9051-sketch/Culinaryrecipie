Israeli Couscous Salad

Toasted pearl couscous with roast vegetables, herbs, lemon and a handful of toasted almonds. Good hot, better at room temperature.

Why This Recipe Works

Toasting the pearls in oil before adding liquid keeps them separate and gives a nutty flavour that plain boiling does not — untoasted ptitim clump into a solid mass as they cool. Dressing the couscous while it is still warm means it absorbs the lemon rather than wearing it.

Method

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Heat the oven to 210C fan. Toss the peppers, courgette, onion and tomatoes with the olive oil, cumin and salt and roast 25 minutes until charred at the edges.

  2. Meanwhile, heat the 3 tablespoons of olive oil in a saucepan and add the dry couscous.

  3. Toast over medium heat for 4 to 5 minutes, stirring constantly, until the pearls are golden and smell like popcorn.

  4. Pour in the hot stock — it will hiss — cover, and simmer 8 to 10 minutes until the liquid is absorbed and the pearls are tender with a slight bite.

  5. Whisk all the dressing ingredients together.

  6. Tip the hot couscous into a wide bowl and pour half the dressing over immediately, forking it through.

  7. Fold in the roast vegetables with all their pan juices and let everything cool to room temperature.

  8. Add the remaining dressing, the herbs, almonds and pomegranate seeds, and toss.

  9. Taste and adjust for lemon and salt — it should be sharper than seems right at first.

Chef’s Tips

  • Toast the pearls until they smell like popcorn. Untoasted couscous clumps.
  • Dress it hot, then again cold. Two stages, better absorption.
  • Herbs at the very end so they stay green.

Pairing Suggestions

  • Grilled chicken or halloumi
  • Labneh
  • A crisp white wine

Storage & Reheating

Refrigerate up to 3 days. Bring back to room temperature and refresh with lemon before serving.
