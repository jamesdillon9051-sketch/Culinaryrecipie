Sicilian Arancini

Sicilian rice balls with a molten mozzarella and ragù heart, breadcrumbed and fried until they shatter. Traditionally made from leftover risotto, they are the finest argument for cooking too much rice on purpose.

Why This Recipe Works

Risotto must be fridge-cold before shaping: as it chills, the amylopectin retrogrades and the rice sets firm enough to hold a sphere under frying. A double crumb — flour, egg, then breadcrumbs — creates a moisture-proof shell so the cheese melts inside while the crust stays crisp.

Method

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Make a firm risotto: sweat the onion in the oil, toast the rice for 2 minutes, then add hot stock a ladle at a time with the saffron until the rice is just cooked and quite dry, about 16 minutes. Beat in the butter, Parmigiano and salt.

  2. Spread the rice on a tray, cover, and refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight. It must be completely cold and stiff.

  3. Take a heaped tablespoon of rice, flatten it in your damp palm, and press a hollow in the centre. Fill with a teaspoon of ragù, a mozzarella cube and a few peas.

  4. Add more rice on top and roll gently into a smooth ball or the traditional cone shape, about 80 g each. Seal any cracks with wet fingers.

  5. Roll each ball in flour, then beaten egg, then breadcrumbs, pressing firmly. For extra insurance, repeat the egg and crumb.

  6. Chill the coated arancini for 30 minutes to set the crust.

  7. Heat the oil to 175°C / 350°F. Fry three or four at a time for 4 to 5 minutes, turning, until deep golden brown all over.

  8. Drain on a rack and rest for 3 minutes before eating — the centre is molten and will scald.

Chef’s Tips

  • Keep a bowl of water beside you and wet your hands often; the rice will not stick.
  • If a ball cracks in the oil the cheese escapes. When in doubt, double-crumb it.
  • Test the oil with a pinch of breadcrumbs: they should sizzle steadily, not violently.

Pairing Suggestions

  • A cold Sicilian Grillo or a light beer
  • Serve with a bowl of warm marinara for dipping
  • Follow with a bitter leaf salad

Storage & Reheating

Shaped uncooked arancini freeze for 2 months — fry from frozen at 165°C / 330°F for 7 minutes. Cooked ones reheat in an oven at 190°C / 375°F for 12 minutes.
