Chia Pudding

Chia seeds left in milk overnight until they swell into a set, tapioca-like pudding. Two ingredients, five minutes of work, and it does the rest of the job while you sleep.

★★★★★★★★★★4.6(1,622)Rated 4.6 out of 5 from 1622 reviews 8 min total Serves 2 servings Easy
Jump to Recipe Method

Why This Recipe Works

Chia seeds absorb roughly ten times their weight in liquid, forming a gel around each seed — the ratio of about one part seed to five parts liquid is what gives a spoonable set rather than a soup or a solid block. Stirring twice in the first half hour prevents the seeds clumping into lumps that never hydrate.

Method

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Whisk the milk, maple syrup, vanilla and salt together in a bowl or jar until the syrup has dissolved.

  2. Whisk in any flavouring — cocoa, lemon or cinnamon — until completely smooth.

  3. Add the chia seeds and whisk hard for 30 seconds so they are fully dispersed and not clumping.

  4. Leave for 10 minutes, then whisk again. This second stir is what prevents lumps.

  5. Leave another 20 minutes and stir a third time.

  6. Cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours, ideally overnight.

  7. In the morning, check the set: it should hold a spoon shape. Loosen with a splash of milk if it is too stiff, or add a teaspoon more chia and wait an hour if too loose.

  8. Stir in the yoghurt if using for a creamier texture.

  9. Divide between glasses or jars, and top with fruit, nuts, nut butter and granola just before eating.

Chef’s Tips

  • Stir at least twice in the first half hour, or you will find clumps of dry seed in the middle.
  • One part seeds to five parts liquid by volume is the reliable ratio to remember.
  • Make five jars on Sunday and breakfast is settled for the week.

Pairing Suggestions

  • Coffee or green tea
  • Fresh fruit
  • A handful of toasted nuts

Storage & Reheating

Refrigerate up to 5 days, covered. Add toppings only when serving so they stay crisp. Do not freeze.
