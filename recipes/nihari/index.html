Nihari

Beef shank stewed overnight in a spice-heavy gravy thickened with wheat flour, finished with ginger, chilli and lime. Breakfast in Old Delhi and Karachi.

Why This Recipe Works

Nihari is one of very few curries thickened with a flour slurry, and it has to be whisked into hot liquid off the boil or it sets into lumps that never disperse. The marrow bones are not garnish: the marrow melting into the gravy is what gives nihari its particular richness, and it is scooped out and stirred in at the end.

Method

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Toast all the whole masala spices in a dry pan for 90 seconds until fragrant, then grind to a powder with the dried ginger, nutmeg and mace.

  2. Heat the ghee in a very heavy pot and fry the onions for 12 minutes until deep golden. Lift half out for the garnish.

  3. Add the ginger-garlic paste and cook 2 minutes.

  4. Add the beef and marrow bones and brown for 10 minutes.

  5. Add the chilli powder, turmeric, salt and two-thirds of the ground masala, and fry 3 minutes.

  6. Pour in the hot water, bring to a simmer, cover, and cook on the lowest possible heat for 4 to 5 hours, or overnight in a very low oven at 120C. The beef should be falling apart.

  7. Lift the meat and bones out. Bring the gravy to a bare simmer and take it off the boil.

  8. Whisk the flour and cold water slurry until completely smooth, then pour it into the pot in a thin stream while whisking hard.

  9. Return to low heat and cook 15 to 20 minutes, stirring, until the gravy thickens and turns glossy and a slick of ghee rises.

  10. Return the beef, scoop the marrow out of the bones and stir it in, and add the rest of the masala.

  11. Serve topped with the reserved fried onions, ginger matchsticks, green chilli, coriander and lime, with naan.

Chef’s Tips

  • Whisk the flour slurry into gravy that is off the boil. Lumps are permanent.
  • The overnight cook is traditional and it does show. Four hours is the minimum.
  • Stir the marrow in. It is where a lot of the richness comes from.

Pairing Suggestions

  • Naan or khameeri roti
  • Lime and green chilli
  • Sweet lassi

Storage & Reheating

Refrigerate up to 4 days, better on day two, and freeze 3 months.
