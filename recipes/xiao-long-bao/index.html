Xiao Long Bao

Shanghai soup dumplings: pleated parcels holding pork and a mouthful of hot broth, created by folding chilled aspic into the filling so it melts as they steam. Eighteen pleats is the traditional standard.

Why This Recipe Works

The soup is not injected — it is a rich, gelatine-heavy stock set solid, diced and mixed into the raw pork, which liquefies in the steamer. The wrapper is rolled thin at the edges and left thicker at the centre so the base can hold the weight of hot liquid without splitting.

Method

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Blanch the pork skin for 5 minutes, drain and scrape clean. Simmer it with the water, ginger, spring onion and wine for 2½ hours until the liquid is sticky between your fingers.

  2. Strain into a shallow tray and refrigerate until set firm, at least 4 hours. Chop the set aspic into small dice.

  3. Mix the pork with the soy, wine, sugar, sesame oil, ginger, spring onion and white pepper, stirring vigorously in one direction for 3 minutes until sticky and paste-like.

  4. Fold in the diced aspic gently and refrigerate the filling for 30 minutes.

  5. For the dough, stir the boiled water into the flour with chopsticks, then the cold water, and knead 8 minutes to a smooth, firm dough. Rest, covered, 30 minutes.

  6. Roll the dough into a rope and cut into 24 pieces. Roll each into an 8 cm circle, thin at the edge and thicker in the middle.

  7. Place a heaped teaspoon of filling in the centre, then pleat around the rim, gathering and twisting the pleats into a spiral, and pinch the top closed.

  8. Line a bamboo steamer with cabbage leaves or paper, space the dumplings 2 cm apart, and steam over vigorously boiling water for 8 minutes.

  9. Serve immediately with black vinegar and ginger. Warn everyone to bite a small hole first and drink the soup before eating the rest.

Chef’s Tips

  • Keep the filling cold. Warm aspic softens and the dumplings become impossible to pleat.
  • Do not overfill. A heaped teaspoon looks stingy and is exactly right — more and the base tears in the steamer.
  • Freeze uncooked dumplings on a tray, then bag them. Steam from frozen for 11 minutes.

Pairing Suggestions

  • Black vinegar with ginger shreds, the only correct dip
  • Jasmine or oolong tea
  • Blanched greens

Storage & Reheating

Best straight from the steamer. Uncooked dumplings freeze for 2 months; cooked ones lose their soup and are not worth keeping.
