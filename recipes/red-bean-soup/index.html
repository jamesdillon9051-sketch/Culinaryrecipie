Red Bean Soup

Adzuki beans cooked to collapse with rock sugar and dried tangerine peel, eaten warm or cold. Barely sweet, and closer to a soup than a pudding.

1 hr 40 min prep and cooking plus 8 hr soaking the adzuki beans overnight 9 hr 40 min start to finish Serves 6 servings Easy

Storing and Planning Ahead

Refrigerated for 5 days and it thickens. Freezes 3 months.

Making Red Bean Soup

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Soak the adzuki beans in cold water 8 hours, then drain and rinse them.

  2. Soak the tangerine peel in warm water 15 minutes, then scrape the white pith off the inside with a spoon.

  3. Put the beans, the 2 litres of water, the tangerine peel and the pandan leaves in a pot and bring to the boil.

  4. Simmer, half covered, 60 minutes.

  5. Add the lotus seeds if using and simmer 30 minutes more, until the beans have collapsed and the liquid has thickened.

  6. Crush some of the beans against the side of the pot to thicken it further.

  7. Only now add the rock sugar and the salt, and simmer 10 minutes until dissolved.

  8. Serve warm, or chill and serve cold with a spoonful of coconut milk.

Worth Knowing

  • Sugar at the end. Early sugar stops the beans softening.
  • Scrape the pith from the tangerine peel or it turns bitter.
  • Crush some of the beans to thicken it.

Why the Method Is What It Is

Sugar goes in at the end, never at the start. Sugar in the cooking water slows the beans down dramatically — it draws water out of them by osmosis — and beans cooked in sweetened liquid can simmer for hours and still be firm. Cook them in plain water until they collapse, then sweeten. Dried tangerine peel is the traditional aromatic and it does more than it looks like it should: soak it and scrape the bitter white pith off the inside first.

Good Alongside

Coconut milk · Sesame balls · Served cold in summer

Questions About Red Bean Soup

How long does Red Bean Soup take to make?

10 minutes of preparation and 1 hour 30 minutes of cooking, plus 8 hours of soaking the adzuki beans overnight, so allow 9 hours 40 minutes from start to finish. The recipe serves 6.
