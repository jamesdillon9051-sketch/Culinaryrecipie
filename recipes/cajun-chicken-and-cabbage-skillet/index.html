Cajun Chicken and Cabbage Skillet

Cubes of chicken coated in a homemade Cajun spice blend and cooked with cabbage, onion and pepper in one pan. Four servings in 30 minutes.

Cabbage releases a lot of water, and in a crowded pan it turns a skillet into a stew. The fix is simple. Brown the chicken first, then cook the cabbage in handfuls and uncovered, so the water cooks off instead of pooling. That one change keeps the flavour concentrated.

The seasoning is a homemade Cajun blend of paprika, garlic and onion powders, oregano, thyme, cayenne and pepper, so you control the salt and the heat. Keep the cayenne small and add more at the table; its heat builds as it cooks. Heat is up to you. Half the blend goes on the chicken and half goes into the vegetables, so every layer is seasoned.

Peppers and onion add sweetness that balances the spice, and a squeeze of lemon at the end brightens it. Cabbage keeps its bite if you stop the cooking when it is just tender. Serve straight from the pan.

What People Ask

How long does Cajun Chicken and Cabbage Skillet take to make?

About 30 minutes in total — 10 minutes of preparation and 20 minutes of cooking. The recipe serves 4.

What are the key tips for making Cajun Chicken and Cabbage Skillet?

Slice the cabbage thinly so it cooks in the time the chicken takes. If you like more heat, add a pinch more cayenne at the end rather than at the start. Use a pan big enough to hold the cabbage in a loose pile; a crowded pan steams it.

What should I serve with Cajun Chicken and Cabbage Skillet?

Cauliflower rice; A small portion of brown rice; Lemon wedges.

How should Cajun Chicken and Cabbage Skillet be stored?

Keeps in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat in a hot pan.

The Method for Cajun Chicken and Cabbage Skillet

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Mix the paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, oregano, thyme, cayenne, black pepper and salt in a bowl. Toss the chicken in half of the spice mix.

  2. Heat the oil in a large, deep frying pan over medium-high heat. Cook the chicken for 5 minutes, turning, until browned and nearly cooked through. Lift out onto a plate.

  3. Add the onion and pepper and cook for 3 minutes. Add the cabbage in handfuls, the garlic and the rest of the spice mix and cook for 5 minutes, stirring, until the cabbage begins to wilt.

  4. Pour in the stock, return the chicken to the pan and cook uncovered for 4 minutes, until the cabbage is tender and the liquid has mostly gone. Stir in the lemon juice and scatter with the spring onions.

A Few Pointers

  • Slice the cabbage thinly so it cooks in the time the chicken takes.
  • If you like more heat, add a pinch more cayenne at the end rather than at the start.
  • Use a pan big enough to hold the cabbage in a loose pile; a crowded pan steams it.

If You're Missing Something

  • Out of spring onions? A small regular onion, finely sliced, or a few chives stirred in at the end both cover the job.
  • An eighth of a teaspoon of garlic powder per clove is the usual stand-in when you are out of fresh garlic — it will not caramelise the same way, but it carries the flavour.
  • Vegetable stock in place of the chicken stock makes this meat-free without changing the method, if that is what you need.

What to Serve With It

Cauliflower rice · A small portion of brown rice · Lemon wedges

Keeping and Reheating

Keeps in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat in a hot pan.
