The Case for Mandazi

Roll them thicker than feels right — 5 mm, not 3 — and cut generous triangles. Thin mandazi crisp through and turn into biscuits; the thing you want is a puffed pillow with a hollow inside and a soft crumb, and only a thick piece of dough traps enough steam to blow itself open like that in the first thirty seconds in the oil.

How to Cook Mandazi

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, cardamom and salt together.

  2. Beat the egg with the coconut milk and melted butter.

  3. Pour the wet into the dry and bring it together into a soft dough.

  4. Knead 5 minutes on a lightly floured surface, until smooth.

  5. Cover and rest it 20 minutes.

  6. Roll out to 5 mm thick and cut into squares, then cut each square into two triangles.

  7. Heat the oil in a deep pan to 170C.

  8. Fry in batches of four for 3 minutes, until puffed and golden underneath.

  9. Turn and fry 2 minutes more, then lift onto a rack.

  10. Dust with icing sugar and serve warm.

Worth Knowing Before You Cook

Five millimetres. Thinner and they will not puff.

Coconut milk, not water — it is what makes the crumb tender.

Fry the offcuts first to test the oil.

Leftovers

Best the day they are made. Keep in a tin 2 days and warm through before serving.
