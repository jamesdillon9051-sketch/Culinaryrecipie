Beef Empanadas

Argentinian empanadas with a juicy, gently spiced beef filling, olives and egg, baked in a short pastry that stays crisp. Chilling the filling overnight is the trick to a juicy centre without a leaking crust.

★★★★★★★★★★4.8(2,133)Rated 4.8 out of 5 from 2133 reviews 1 hr 5 min total Serves 12 servings Medium
Jump to Recipe Method

Why This Recipe Works

The filling is deliberately made loose and then chilled overnight, so the fat sets solid and can be sealed inside easily — but melts during baking to baste the meat, giving that juicy centre. Warm filling would soften the dough and leak through every seam.

Method

Tap a step to highlight it, or turn on Cook Mode for large type and step-by-step focus.

  1. Rub the butter into the flour and salt until it resembles coarse crumbs. Beat the egg into the cold water and add gradually, bringing it together into a firm dough. Chill 1 hour.

  2. Cook the onions in the olive oil over medium-low heat for 15 minutes until soft and sweet.

  3. Raise the heat, add the beef and brown for 8 minutes, breaking it up.

  4. Add the paprika, cumin, oregano, chilli flakes and salt and cook 2 minutes, then the stock. Simmer 5 minutes — the mixture should be moist, not dry.

  5. Cool completely, then refrigerate overnight. This is the step that makes the difference.

  6. Fold the chopped egg, olives and spring onions into the cold filling.

  7. Roll the dough to 3 mm and cut 13 cm circles.

  8. Place 2 tablespoons of filling on each, brush the rim with water, fold into a half-moon and press firmly to seal.

  9. Crimp the edge with the traditional repulgue twist, or press with a fork.

  10. Brush with beaten egg and bake at 200°C / 390°F for 22 to 25 minutes until deep golden. Rest 5 minutes before eating — the filling is molten.

Chef’s Tips

  • Chill the filling overnight. A warm or even room-temperature filling will leak and soften the pastry.
  • Seal firmly and completely; any gap becomes a blowout in the oven.
  • Chopped hard-boiled egg is traditional and adds richness — do not leave it out.

Pairing Suggestions

  • A Malbec, naturally, or a cold lager
  • Serve with chimichurri for dipping
  • Follow with dulce de leche pancakes

Storage & Reheating

Unbaked empanadas freeze 3 months — bake from frozen, adding 8 minutes. Baked ones keep 3 days; re-crisp at 190°C for 10 minutes.
