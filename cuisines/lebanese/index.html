Lemon, parsley and a table covered in small plates. 52 Lebanese recipes, from the everyday to the ambitious.

Of these, 7 are ready in 30 minutes or less, start to finish; the quickest are Lebanese Cabbage Salad (10 min), Carrot and Tahini Salad (15 min) and Shanklish Salad (15 min).
