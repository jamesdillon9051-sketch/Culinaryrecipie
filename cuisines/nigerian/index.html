Nigerian recipes

Pepper bases cooked down hard, and rice worth arguing over. 16 Nigerian recipes, from the everyday to the ambitious.

The 16 recipes cover 5 of the site’s categories, among them 10 dinners, 2 appetisers and small plates, 2 lunches, 1 breakfast and 1 dessert. Of these, one is ready in 30 minutes or less, start to finish. For 6, the method times a wait such as rising, chilling or marinating, and the time at the top of the recipe includes it. By difficulty: 7 easy and 9 medium.

As written, 6 are vegetarian, 5 vegan, 6 gluten-free and 11 dairy-free. Another 4 meet a diet label only with a swap the recipe names, and the recipe says which.
