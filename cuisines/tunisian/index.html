Tunisian recipes

Harissa, eggs and pastry folded around both. 40 Tunisian recipes, from the everyday to the ambitious.

The 40 recipes cover 9 of the site’s categories, among them 14 dinners, 7 desserts, 6 appetisers and small plates, 4 breakfasts, 4 lunches and 2 bakes. Of these, 4 are ready in 30 minutes or less, start to finish; the quickest are Slata Tounsia (20 min), Thé aux Pignons (25 min) and Ojja (30 min). For 16, the method times a wait such as rising, chilling or marinating, and the time at the top of the recipe includes it. By difficulty: 20 easy, 18 medium and 2 hard.
