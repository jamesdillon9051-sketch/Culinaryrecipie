Western Europe

Best places to visit in France

Alpine summits, Atlantic oyster beds, Roman ruins and the world's most visited city — inside one very well-connected country.

1
Major CityCapital City

Paris

Île-de-France

France's capital packs more world-class museums, monuments and neighbourhood restaurants into six square miles than anywhere else in Europe. It rewards walking more than ticking off a list.

Don't miss The Louvre, the Musée d'Orsay and Sainte-Chapelle — three of Europe's great collections within one Métro ride of each other.

Explore Destination → — Paris
2
Scenic RegionFood & Wine

Provence & the Luberon

Vaucluse, Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur

A landscape of ochre cliffs, perched stone villages and vineyards between the Rhône and the Alps, with the best-preserved Roman monuments outside Italy on its western edge.

Don't miss The Pont du Gard, a three-tier Roman aqueduct that has stood for two thousand years without mortar.

Explore Destination → — Provence & the Luberon
3
Coast & BeachesMajor City

Nice & the French Riviera

Alpes-Maritimes, Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur

France's fifth city curves around a two-mile bay of blue water, with an old town that was Italian until 1860 and a local rail line linking a dozen coastal villages.

Don't miss The Cours Saleya flower market, then the coastal train to Villefranche, Èze and Menton for €2 a hop.

Explore Destination → — Nice & the French Riviera
4
Historic SiteUNESCO Site

The Loire Valley

Centre-Val de Loire

France's royal playground for two centuries: Renaissance châteaux, formal gardens and crisp white wine strung along 170 miles of the country's longest river, an hour from Paris.

Don't miss Chenonceau, arched on five spans across the River Cher — the most beautiful building in France by most reckonings.

Explore Destination → — The Loire Valley
5
UNESCO SiteHistoric Site

Mont-Saint-Michel

Manche, Normandy

A rock in a vast tidal bay, crowned by an abbey that took five centuries to build. Some three million people visit a year, and almost all of them leave before the best hours.

Don't miss The abbey after the day-trippers leave, when the village empties and the bay goes silent.

Explore Destination → — Mont-Saint-Michel
6
MountainsNature & Wildlife

Chamonix & Mont Blanc

Haute-Savoie, Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes

A valley town beneath a 4,808-metre summit, where glaciers reach almost to the treeline and a network of lifts puts high-alpine terrain within reach of anyone.

Don't miss The Aiguille du Midi: a rock spire with a viewing terrace at 3,842 m, reached by the highest vertical-ascent cable car in the world.

Explore Destination → — Chamonix & Mont Blanc
7
Major CityFood & Wine

Bordeaux

Gironde, Nouvelle-Aquitaine

An 18th-century city of pale limestone facades along a curving river, transformed over two decades from soot-blackened port to one of France's most liveable cities.

Don't miss The Miroir d'Eau opposite the Place de la Bourse: the world's largest reflecting pool, and the best free show in France.

Explore Destination → — Bordeaux
8
Historic SiteCoast & Beaches

Normandy & the D-Day Beaches

Calvados and Manche, Normandy

Five beaches, a dozen museums, cliff-top batteries and immaculately kept cemeteries, in a green farming region that also gave the world Camembert, cider and Calvados.

Don't miss The Normandy American Cemetery above Omaha Beach — 9,388 graves in white marble, looking down on the sand.

Explore Destination → — Normandy & the D-Day Beaches
9
Culture & ArtsUNESCO Site

Strasbourg & Alsace

Bas-Rhin and Haut-Rhin, Grand Est

A region that changed nationality four times in seventy-five years and kept both cultures — French administration, German architecture, and a food and wine tradition that belongs to neither.

Don't miss Strasbourg Cathedral's astronomical clock and the 142-metre spire you can climb for a view into three countries.

Explore Destination → — Strasbourg & Alsace
10
Historic SiteNature & Wildlife

The Dordogne & Sarlat

Dordogne, Nouvelle-Aquitaine

A limestone valley of castles, walnut groves and honey-coloured villages, holding the densest concentration of prehistoric sites in Europe and France's richest regional cooking.

Don't miss Lascaux — the most sophisticated prehistoric art ever found, seen through a replica so exact you forget it is one.

Explore Destination → — The Dordogne & Sarlat

Southern Europe

Best places to visit in Spain

Moorish palaces, Atlantic surf, volcanic islands and a capital that eats at eleven — four distinct cultures inside one border.

1
Major CityCoast & Beaches

Barcelona

Catalonia

Catalonia's capital pairs a dense Gothic core with the most ambitious modernista architecture in Europe, and puts a city beach at the end of the metro line.

Don't miss The Sagrada Família's stained glass at the right hour — blue-green in the morning, red-gold by late afternoon.

Explore Destination → — Barcelona
2
UNESCO SiteHistoric Site

Granada

Andalusia

A hillside city beneath the Sierra Nevada, holding the Alhambra — the finest surviving Islamic palace complex anywhere — and a tapas culture where the food still comes free with the drink.

Don't miss The Nasrid Palaces: honeycomb vaulting, carved stucco and courtyards designed entirely around water and light.

Explore Destination → — Granada
3
Major CityCulture & Arts

Seville

Andalusia

The heart of southern Spain, where flamenco, tapas and Holy Week processions are living traditions rather than performances, wrapped around a medieval core of white walls and orange trees.

Don't miss The Real Alcázar — an Islamic palace rebuilt by Christian kings in the same style, and still a working royal residence.

Explore Destination → — Seville
4
Capital CityMajor City

Madrid

Community of Madrid

Spain's capital sits at 650 m on the Castilian plateau, with an art triangle no other European city matches and a nightlife that genuinely does not start until midnight.

Don't miss The Prado's Velázquez rooms — Las Meninas hangs where it can be walked around, in the light it was painted for.

Explore Destination → — Madrid
5
Coast & BeachesFood & Wine

San Sebastián & the Basque Country

Gipuzkoa, Basque Country

Green, Atlantic and wet, the Basque Country is the Spain nobody expects: a belle-époque resort on a perfect bay, backed by mountains and the best food culture in the country.

Don't miss Pintxos in the Parte Vieja — bar counters loaded with food, paid for by the toothpick, four or five bars an evening.

Explore Destination → — San Sebastián & the Basque Country
6
UNESCO SiteHistoric Site

Córdoba

Andalusia

A compact Andalusian city on the Guadalquivir holding one of the most astonishing interiors in the world, plus flower-filled patios and Spain's best cold soup.

Don't miss The Mezquita's hypostyle hall — 856 columns under red-and-white double arches, with a cathedral built into the middle of it.

Explore Destination → — Córdoba
7
Major CityCoast & Beaches

Valencia

Valencian Community

A Mediterranean city with a Gothic old town, Europe's most striking modern architectural complex, a city beach and the best-value eating of any major Spanish city.

Don't miss The Turia — a river diverted after a flood and turned into a green park running right through the middle of the city.

Explore Destination → — Valencia
8
IslandCoast & Beaches

Mallorca

Balearic Islands

The largest Balearic island has a proper capital city, a mountain range along its entire northwest coast, and hundreds of coves that never appear in the package-holiday version of it.

Don't miss The Serra de Tramuntana — a UNESCO-listed mountain range of terraced olive groves dropping straight into the sea.

Explore Destination → — Mallorca
9
IslandNature & Wildlife

Tenerife & Mount Teide

Canary Islands

A volcanic island 1,000 km from mainland Spain, with year-round warmth, a black-sand coastline and a national park caldera that looks like another planet.

Don't miss The cable car to 3,555 m on Teide, above the cloud layer, looking down on the other Canary Islands.

Explore Destination → — Tenerife & Mount Teide
10
UNESCO SiteCulture & Arts

Santiago de Compostela & Galicia

Galicia

A granite cathedral city at the end of Europe's oldest pilgrimage route, in a region of rías, oyster beds and rainfall that looks and tastes nothing like the rest of Spain.

Don't miss The moment pilgrims walk into the Praza do Obradoiro after weeks on the Camino — worth seeing even if you arrived by train.

Explore Destination → — Santiago de Compostela & Galicia

North America

Best places to visit in the United States

A canyon a mile deep, a city built on jazz, a supervolcano full of wildlife and 3,000 miles of country between them.

1
Major CityCulture & Arts

New York City

New York

The densest, most walkable American city, where five distinct boroughs, a 24-hour subway and a century of immigration produce something no other city in the country resembles.

Don't miss The Metropolitan Museum — two million works across 5,000 years, and you could go every day for a month.

Explore Destination → — New York City
2
National ParkNature & Wildlife

Grand Canyon National Park

Arizona

The single most overwhelming landscape in North America: a canyon so large the eye cannot process it, with a river a vertical mile below and rock at the bottom half as old as the earth.

Don't miss Sunrise or sunset from the rim, when shadow moves across the buttes and the whole thing stops looking flat.

Explore Destination → — Grand Canyon National Park
3
Major CityCoast & Beaches

San Francisco & the California Coast

California

The most walkable major city on the American West Coast, with cable cars, Victorian terraces and a food culture built on Californian produce — plus redwoods, wine country and Highway 1 within two hours.

Don't miss Walking or cycling the Golden Gate Bridge, with the fog moving through the towers beneath you.

Explore Destination → — San Francisco & the California Coast
4
Major CityCulture & Arts

New Orleans

Louisiana

A city that was French, then Spanish, then French again before it was American, and which turned that inheritance into a food and music culture found nowhere else on the continent.

Don't miss Live brass and jazz on Frenchmen Street, seven nights a week, with no cover charge in half the rooms.

Explore Destination → — New Orleans
5
National ParkNature & Wildlife

Yellowstone National Park

Wyoming, Montana and Idaho

Nearly 9,000 square kilometres of high volcanic plateau in Wyoming, holding over 10,000 thermal features, grizzly bears, wolves, and herds of bison that stop the traffic.

Don't miss Wolves and bison in the Lamar Valley at dawn — the closest thing to an African game drive in North America.

Explore Destination → — Yellowstone National Park
6
Capital CityCulture & Arts

Washington, D.C.

District of Columbia

A planned capital of low neoclassical buildings and wide avenues, holding the largest museum complex in the world and charging nothing to enter almost any of it.

Don't miss The National Air and Space Museum and the National Museum of African American History and Culture — free, and among the best of their kind anywhere.

Explore Destination → — Washington, D.C.
7
National ParkNature & Wildlife

Zion & Southern Utah

Utah

A concentration of canyon country unmatched anywhere: Zion's sheer red walls, Bryce's hoodoos, Arches' sandstone spans and Canyonlands' mesas, within a few hours of each other.

Don't miss The Narrows — wading up the Virgin River between sandstone walls 600 metres high and, in places, ten metres apart.

Explore Destination → — Zion & Southern Utah
8
Major CityCulture & Arts

Chicago

Illinois

America's great architectural city, on a lake the size of a small country, with a museum of Impressionism to rival Paris and the blues clubs that shaped modern music.

Don't miss The architecture boat tour on the Chicago River — the best hour of sightseeing in any American city.

Explore Destination → — Chicago
9
IslandCoast & Beaches

Maui, Hawai'i

Hawai'i

The second-largest Hawaiian island, with a summit above the clouds, a rainforest coast road, some of the Pacific's best snorkelling and a winter whale population in the thousands.

Don't miss Sunrise above the clouds from Haleakalā's summit crater, at 3,055 m, in near-freezing air.

Explore Destination → — Maui, Hawai'i
10
Historic SiteCoast & Beaches

Charleston, South Carolina

South Carolina

A walkable colonial port with the best-preserved historic centre in the United States, an outstanding food scene, and a history of slavery that the city has increasingly stopped avoiding.

Don't miss The pastel houses of Rainbow Row and the double piazzas of Church Street, walked at 08:00 with nobody about.

Explore Destination → — Charleston, South Carolina
