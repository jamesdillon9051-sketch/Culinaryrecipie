# Flickr photograph round (21 October 2026)

26 recipe photographs added from Flickr's public commercial-use CC and public-domain search, after the per-photo page was fetched and its licence link and photographer name matched the search record (26 of 26). Each was looked at at full size against the recipe and its diet labels. Not cooking evidence: these are other people's photographs of the dish.

- Searches run: 512 of the 1,139 recipes still without a picture (alphabetical, to 'green'); the run stopped when Flickr's known-positive control returned an empty search model. It was not worked around. The remaining recipes were not searched on Flickr in this pass. 25 searches were unverified.
- 316 recipes had candidates; the top two for each (573 downloads) were screened on contact sheets, 41 reviewed at full size, 26 adopted.
- Rejected after full-size review for a mismatch with the recipe or its labels, among others: a photograph with a wheat product on a Gluten-Free recipe (charoset with matzo, eggs Sardou with toast), cheese on a Dairy-Free recipe (green chile stew), a pizza crust for crustless Eggplant and Portobello Pizzas, raw or uncooked assemblies, restaurant signage and people, and Piersey.com images (AI-looking, not adopted).
- Selected photographs, licences and differences: [selected.csv](selected.csv). Keep the credits in images-attribution.md.
- 1,113 recipes still have no picture.
