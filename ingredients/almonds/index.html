Almonds recipes

Flaked, ground and whole. 211 recipes on the site call for almonds.

Almonds is in 211 recipes from 32 cuisines, among them 54 desserts, 53 bakes, 36 dinners and 25 holiday dishes. The cuisines that use it most here are American (47), French (26), British (20) and Indian (18). Of these, 36 are ready in 30 minutes or less, start to finish; the quickest are Affogato (5 min), Chocolate Mug Cake (5 min) and Lemon Arugula Salad with Parmesan (5 min). For 114, the method times a wait such as rising, chilling or marinating, and the time at the top of the recipe includes it. By difficulty: 110 easy, 76 medium and 25 hard.

As written, 166 are vegetarian, 16 vegan, 85 gluten-free and 38 dairy-free. Another 15 meet a diet label only with a swap the recipe names, and the recipe says which.
