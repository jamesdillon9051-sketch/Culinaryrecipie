Honey recipes

Sweetening, glazing and drizzling. 213 recipes on the site call for honey.

Honey is in 213 recipes from 36 cuisines, among them 46 dinners, 32 bakes, 22 appetisers and small plates and 22 healthy dishes. The cuisines that use it most here are American (92), Japanese (13), Italian (12) and British (11). Of these, 81 are ready in 30 minutes or less, start to finish; the quickest are Banana Smoothie (3 min), Bee's Knees (4 min) and Banana Sandwich (5 min). For 92, the method times a wait such as rising, chilling or marinating, and the time at the top of the recipe includes it. By difficulty: 154 easy, 47 medium and 12 hard.

As written, 132 are vegetarian, 53 gluten-free and 82 dairy-free. Another 4 meet a diet label only with a swap the recipe names, and the recipe says which.
