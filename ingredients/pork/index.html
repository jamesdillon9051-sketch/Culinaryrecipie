Pork recipes

Belly, shoulder and chop, from Sunday roasts to street food. 418 recipes on the site call for pork.

Lean cuts dry out quickly, so the methods give a temperature or a cue for when to stop; fatty cuts such as belly and shoulder take the opposite approach and want time. Sausages, bacon and ham turn up here too, in breakfasts, pasta and soups.

Pork is in 418 recipes from 54 cuisines, among them 287 dinners, 43 appetisers and small plates, 37 lunches and 24 breakfasts. The cuisines that use it most here are American (84), Chinese (50), Italian (43) and British (38). Of these, 60 are ready in 30 minutes or less, start to finish; the quickest are Air Fryer Sausages (14 min), Grilled Sausages (17 min) and Pan Fried Pork Chops (17 min). For 154, the method times a wait such as rising, chilling or marinating, and the time at the top of the recipe includes it. By difficulty: 228 easy, 154 medium and 36 hard.

As written, 99 are gluten-free and 214 dairy-free. Another 7 meet a diet label only with a swap the recipe names, and the recipe says which.
