Peppers recipes

Roasted until the skins blister, or raw and sweet. 329 recipes on the site call for peppers.

Peppers is in 329 recipes from 45 cuisines, among them 192 dinners, 35 appetisers and small plates, 31 healthy dishes and 28 lunches. The cuisines that use it most here are American (104), Chinese (27), Spanish (24) and Mexican (21). Of these, 76 are ready in 30 minutes or less, start to finish; the quickest are Hummus Veggie Wrap (10 min), Roasted Red Pepper Hummus (10 min) and Shopska Salad (10 min). For 70, the method times a wait such as rising, chilling or marinating, and the time at the top of the recipe includes it. By difficulty: 250 easy, 75 medium and 4 hard.

As written, 111 are vegetarian, 57 vegan, 135 gluten-free and 185 dairy-free. Another 21 meet a diet label only with a swap the recipe names, and the recipe says which.
