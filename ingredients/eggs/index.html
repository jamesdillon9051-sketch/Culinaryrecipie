Eggs recipes

Breakfasts, custards, batters and the dishes built entirely on them. 1738 recipes on the site call for eggs.

Eggs bind, raise and enrich far more of the site than the egg dishes themselves. Where a recipe serves eggs raw or only lightly cooked, the ingredient list asks for pasteurised eggs.

Eggs is in 1,738 recipes from 83 cuisines, among them 471 bakes, 407 dinners, 281 desserts and 196 breakfasts. The cuisines that use it most here are American (545), British (205), Italian (141) and French (128). Of these, 348 are ready in 30 minutes or less, start to finish; the quickest are Chocolate Mug Cake (5 min), Pisco Sour (5 min) and Snowball Cocktail (5 min). For 800, the method times a wait such as rising, chilling or marinating, and the time at the top of the recipe includes it. By difficulty: 853 easy, 753 medium and 132 hard.

As written, 1,092 are vegetarian, 249 gluten-free and 250 dairy-free. Another 50 meet a diet label only with a swap the recipe names, and the recipe says which.
