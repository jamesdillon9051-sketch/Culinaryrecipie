Bacon recipes

Where a little smoked pork does most of the work. 166 recipes on the site call for bacon.

Bacon is in 166 recipes from 33 cuisines, among them 85 dinners, 31 lunches, 22 breakfasts and 16 appetisers and small plates. The cuisines that use it most here are American (62), British (20), French (17) and German (9). Of these, 39 are ready in 30 minutes or less, start to finish; the quickest are Bloody Mary (8 min), Fried Bread (8 min) and Turkey Club Wrap (10 min). For 36, the method times a wait such as rising, chilling or marinating, and the time at the top of the recipe includes it. By difficulty: 114 easy, 41 medium and 11 hard.

As written, 35 are gluten-free and 48 dairy-free. Another 5 meet a diet label only with a swap the recipe names, and the recipe says which.
