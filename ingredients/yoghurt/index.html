Yoghurt recipes

Marinades, raitas, dressings and breakfasts. 205 recipes on the site call for yoghurt.

Yoghurt is in 205 recipes from 24 cuisines, among them 78 dinners, 28 lunches, 27 appetisers and small plates and 20 bakes. The cuisines that use it most here are Indian (64), American (61), Middle Eastern (13) and Greek (11). Of these, 62 are ready in 30 minutes or less, start to finish; the quickest are Banana Smoothie (3 min), Apple Pie Smoothie (5 min) and Ayran (5 min). For 98, the method times a wait such as rising, chilling or marinating, and the time at the top of the recipe includes it. By difficulty: 133 easy, 58 medium and 14 hard.

As written, 114 are vegetarian and 91 gluten-free. Another 5 meet a diet label only with a swap the recipe names, and the recipe says which.
