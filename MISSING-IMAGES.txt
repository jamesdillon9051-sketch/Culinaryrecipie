Save a photograph as BOTH files in  src/assets/img/recipes/
    <slug>.jpg    800 px wide, quality ~70
    <slug>.webp   800 px wide, quality ~68
Then run:  python3 tools/adopt_images.py   (see its header for the credit and licence it needs)
