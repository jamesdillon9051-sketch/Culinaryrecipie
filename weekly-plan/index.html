Five Dinners for This Week

Five dinners for four people, chosen so the shopping overlaps: the red peppers, broccoli, garlic and parmesan bought for one night are used again on another. Wednesday and Friday go in the oven and need little attention, Monday is a one-pot simmer, and Tuesday and Thursday take 25 minutes on the hob.

The dinners

People at each dinner 4

Each recipe is scaled from the servings it is written for. Cooking times do not change with the quantities; a bigger batch may need a bigger pan or two trays, and a crowded pan cooks more slowly.

How the week runs

  • Shop once for everything on the list. If you buy the cod fresh rather than frozen, buy it nearer Friday.
  • Monday: the chicken and rice is about 55 minutes, most of it simmering with the lid on.
  • Tuesday: put the pasta water on as soon as you are in the kitchen; the broccoli and penne share it.
  • Wednesday: the tray heats in the oven while you cut the peppers and onion; 25 minutes of roasting after that.
  • Thursday: slice the steak and vegetables before the wok goes on; the cooking takes about 10 minutes.
  • Friday: make the slaw and crema while the cod roasts.

Leftovers from any night: refrigerate within 2 hours and follow each recipe's own keeping note.
