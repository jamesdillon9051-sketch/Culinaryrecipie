Dinner for Two

Recipes written for two, so there is nothing to divide and nothing left over unless you want it.

Halving a recipe for four works for some dishes and not others: eggs do not halve neatly, small amounts of sauce burn in a big pan, and a whole fish is a whole fish. Every recipe here is written for two people, with quantities and pan sizes to match.

How these were chosen

  • Written for 2 servings, so no quantity needs scaling.
  • A main course.
  • Where a recipe needs rice or bread that it does not include, the entry says so.

Chosen by reading each recipe's ingredients and method, and checked against the recipe again every time the site is built. Times are the recipes' own figures. None of these recipes has been cooked for this collection yet; how recipes are checked.
