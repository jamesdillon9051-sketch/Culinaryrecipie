Complete Dinners in 30 Minutes

A whole dinner, not a main that still needs a side, on the table within half an hour.

Each of these is a full plate on its own: a protein, a vegetable and a starch cooked together in the recipe. The time on every card is start to finish, so nothing here hides a marinade, a rest or a pot of rice cooked separately.

How these were chosen

  • Prep, cooking and any waiting add up to 30 minutes or less, by the recipe's own times.
  • No step the recipe leaves untimed, and no ingredient that must be cooked in advance (cold cooked rice, for example).
  • Protein, vegetables and a starch are all in the ingredient list, so nothing else needs cooking.
  • A main course, not a side or a snack.

Chosen by reading each recipe's ingredients and method, and checked against the recipe again every time the site is built. Times are the recipes' own figures. None of these recipes has been cooked for this collection yet; how recipes are checked.
