Cook Once, Eat Twice

One larger cook that gives you a second, different dinner within the next three days.

Each entry pairs a recipe that makes more than one meal with a second recipe on the site that is written for the leftovers. The amount to set aside comes from the second recipe's own ingredient list. The keeping advice is the first recipe's own, and the second meal is planned within three days, the general limit the USDA gives for cooked leftovers in the fridge.

How these were chosen

  • The first recipe serves at least four, and its storage note covers keeping it in the fridge.
  • The second recipe already exists on the site and calls for the cooked leftover by weight.
  • The second meal falls within 3 days of the first. Cool leftovers and refrigerate them within 2 hours of cooking.

Chosen by reading each recipe's ingredients and method, and checked against the recipe again every time the site is built. Times are the recipes' own figures. None of these recipes has been cooked for this collection yet; how recipes are checked. Leftover guidance: USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service, "Leftovers and Food Safety".
